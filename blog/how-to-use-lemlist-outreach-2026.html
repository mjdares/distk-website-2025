Email Sequences 2026

Sequence Structure 2026

Effective Lemlist Sequence:

Email 1 (Day 0): Personalized opener
- Dynamic image with prospect's name
- Personalized value proposition
- Soft CTA

Email 2 (Day 3): Value add
- Relevant resource or insight
- Different angle on problem
- Question to engage

Email 3 (Day 6): Social proof
- Case study in their industry
- Results they can relate to
- Clearer meeting request

Email 4 (Day 10): Breakup
- Acknowledge they're busy
- Summarize value
- Final easy CTA

Email Copy Tips 2026

  • Short and scannable: Under 100 words ideal
  • One idea per email: Don't overwhelm
  • Conversational tone: Write like a person
  • Clear CTA: One action to take
  • Mobile friendly: Test on phone
