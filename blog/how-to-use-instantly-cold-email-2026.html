Email Account Infrastructure 2026

Recommended Setup:

Domain Strategy:
- Primary domain: yourcompany.com (don't use for cold email)
- Secondary domains: tryourcompany.com, getourcompany.com
- Use 2-3 mailboxes per domain
- Google Workspace or Microsoft 365

Account Configuration:
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC properly set
- Custom tracking domain
- Professional signatures
- Reasonable daily limits (30-50 emails/account)

Email Warmup 2026

How Instantly Warmup Works 2026

  • Warmup pool: 200,000+ accounts in network
  • Automated sending: Sends/receives on schedule
  • Positive signals: Opens, replies, stars, moves from spam
  • Natural patterns: Mimics human email behavior
  • Continuous: Runs alongside campaigns

Warmup Configuration 2026

Warmup Settings:

New Accounts:
- Start with 1-2 warmup emails/day
- Increase by 2-3 per day
- Target: 30-40 daily warmup emails
- Duration: 2-3 weeks before campaigns

Ongoing:
- Keep warmup enabled during campaigns
- Ratio: ~50% warmup, 50% campaigns
- Monitor deliverability score daily

Warmup Best Practices 2026

  • Don't rush: 2-3 weeks minimum before sending
  • Keep enabled: Never turn off warmup
  • Monitor scores: Watch deliverability metrics
  • Match patterns: Send campaigns during warmup hours
  • Ramp slowly: Gradual volume increases

Content Best Practices 2026

Deliverability-Friendly Emails:

Avoid:
- Spam trigger words (free, guarantee, urgent)
- Too many links (1-2 max)
- Images in cold emails
- HTML formatting
- Attachments in first email
- ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!

Include:
- Plain text formatting
- One clear CTA
- Natural language
- Personalization
- Easy unsubscribe

Monitoring Deliverability 2026

  • Instantly dashboard: Account health scores
  • Open rates: Aim for 40%+ (indicates inbox)
  • Bounce rates: Keep under 3%
  • Spam complaints: Keep near 0%
  • Test tools: Mail-tester, GlockApps

Email Sequences 2026

Effective Sequence Structure 2026

Recommended Sequence:

Email 1 (Day 0): Initial outreach
- Personalized opening
- Value proposition
- Soft CTA (reply to learn more)

Email 2 (Day 3): Follow-up
- Reference first email
- Add new angle/value
- Question-based CTA

Email 3 (Day 7): Case study/social proof
- Relevant success story
- Quantified results
- Meeting request

Email 4 (Day 12): Breakup email
- Acknowledge busy
- Recap value
- Permission to close file

Sequence Best Practices 2026

  • 3-5 emails: Optimal sequence length
  • Spacing: 2-5 days between emails
  • Vary approach: Different angles each email
  • Stop on reply: Remove from sequence automatically
  • A/B test: Test subject lines and CTAs
