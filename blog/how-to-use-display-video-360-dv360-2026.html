What Is Display & Video 360 2026?

Display & Video 360 (DV360) is Google's enterprise-grade demand-side platform (DSP) for programmatic advertising in 2026. As a core component of the Google Marketing Platform, DV360 enables large advertisers and agencies to buy digital advertising inventory across display, video, audio, native, and connected TV (CTV) channels through a unified interface.

Unlike Google Ads, which focuses on Google's owned properties, DV360 provides access to 90+ ad exchanges and premium inventory sources, making it the go-to platform for enterprise media buying and brand awareness campaigns in 2026.

Who Uses DV360 in 2026?

  • Enterprise advertisers with ₹10L+ monthly budgets
  • Media agencies managing multiple large accounts
  • D2C brands running cross-channel campaigns
  • Funded startups scaling brand awareness
  • FMCG and retail companies targeting mass audiences

DV360 Core Modules

DV360 is built on five fundamental modules:

Module Purpose
Campaigns Main dashboard for building and managing ad campaigns
Audiences Tools for demographic, interest, behavioral, and first/third-party data targeting
Creatives Suite for designing and managing ad formats including dynamic creatives
Inventory Access to Google Ad Exchange, third-party exchanges, and private marketplaces
Insights Reporting, analytics, and measurement tools

How DV360 Works: Programmatic Advertising 2026

DV360 enables programmatic advertising—automated buying and selling of digital ad inventory in real-time through auctions. Here's how it works in 2026:

Programmatic Buying Types

Type Description Best For
Real-Time Bidding (RTB) Open auction where advertisers bid for impressions in milliseconds Scale, reach, cost efficiency
Private Marketplace (PMP) Invitation-only auction with premium publishers Brand safety, premium placement
Programmatic Guaranteed Direct deals with guaranteed impressions and fixed pricing Reserved premium inventory
Preferred Deals First-look access at fixed prices before open auction Priority access without commitment

Campaign Hierarchy

DV360 campaigns follow a structured hierarchy:

  1. Partner (top level, typically agency)
  2. Advertiser (brand or client)
  3. Campaign (objective, budget, dates)
  4. Insertion Order (budget allocation, pacing)
  5. Line Item (targeting, bidding, creative assignment)

Key Features & Capabilities 2026

1. Multi-Channel Campaign Management

DV360 enables marketers to manage campaigns across all digital channels from one platform:

  • Display: Banner ads, native, responsive display
  • Video: YouTube, in-stream, out-stream, bumper ads
  • Connected TV (CTV): Smart TV apps, streaming services
  • Audio: Podcast ads, music streaming
  • Native: In-feed ads matching content style
  • Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH): Digital billboards, screens

2. Advanced Audience Targeting

DV360 offers the most advanced targeting options in programmatic advertising 2026:

  • First-party data integration: Customer lists, CRM data, website visitors
  • Google Audience segments: Affinity, in-market, life events
  • Custom intent audiences: Based on search behavior
  • Third-party data: DMP integration for extended reach
  • Contextual targeting: Keywords, topics, placements
  • Cross-device tracking: Google's identity graph for unified view

3. Premium Inventory Access

Unlike Google Ads, DV360 provides access to:

  • YouTube via programmatic buying
  • Google Display Network
  • Google AdX (Ad Exchange)
  • 90+ third-party exchanges
  • Private marketplace deals with premium publishers
  • Connected TV inventory (Hotstar, SonyLIV, etc. in India)

4. Creative Optimization

DV360's creative suite in 2026 includes:

  • Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO): Personalized ads based on user signals
  • Feed-based creative: Product catalog integration
  • Creative Studio integration: Advanced HTML5 and rich media
  • A/B testing: Automated creative testing
  • Optimized creatives: AI-powered asset combinations

5. Advanced Measurement

  • Brand Lift studies: Measure brand awareness impact
  • Active View: Viewability measurement
  • Cross-device measurement: Unified user journey
  • Attribution modeling: Multi-touch attribution
  • Incremental reach analysis: Additional reach beyond TV
  • Foot traffic attribution: Offline store visits

Decision Framework

Choose Google Ads if:

  • Monthly ad budget under ₹5L
  • Primary goal is direct response (leads, sales)
  • Search advertising is core strategy
  • Quick setup and self-service needed
  • Local business targeting

Choose DV360 if:

  • Monthly ad budget over ₹10L
  • Primary goal is brand awareness and reach
  • Need CTV, audio, or DOOH advertising
  • Want premium inventory access (private deals)
  • Require advanced measurement (Brand Lift)
  • Running multi-channel programmatic campaigns

Use Both (Full-Funnel Strategy):

  • DV360 for top-of-funnel awareness (display, video, CTV)
  • Google Ads for bottom-of-funnel conversions (search, shopping)
  • Combined budget over ₹25L monthly

Setting Up DV360 Campaigns 2026

Prerequisites

  1. Google Marketing Platform access (requires application/partner)
  2. Campaign Manager 360 setup for ad serving and tracking
  3. Floodlight tags implemented on website
  4. First-party data uploaded (optional but recommended)
  5. Creative assets prepared (display, video formats)

Step-by-Step Setup

Step 1: Create Advertiser

  • Set up advertiser profile in DV360
  • Link Campaign Manager 360 account
  • Configure conversion tracking
  • Upload first-party audiences

Step 2: Create Campaign

  • Define campaign objective (awareness, reach, conversions)
  • Set flight dates and overall budget
  • Choose frequency cap settings
  • Configure brand safety controls

Step 3: Create Insertion Order

  • Allocate budget from campaign
  • Set pacing (daily, flight, ASAP)
  • Define performance goals
  • Configure optimization settings

Step 4: Create Line Items

  • Select inventory source (open exchange, PMP, etc.)
  • Configure audience targeting
  • Set bidding strategy and bid amount
  • Assign creative assets
  • Define frequency caps at line item level

Step 5: Launch and Monitor

  • Review all settings
  • Submit for launch
  • Monitor initial pacing and delivery
  • Optimize based on performance

FAQs: Display & Video 360 2026

What is Display & Video 360 (DV360)?

Display & Video 360 is Google's enterprise-grade demand-side platform (DSP) for programmatic advertising. It enables advertisers to buy display, video, audio, native, and connected TV inventory across Google properties and 90+ ad exchanges through a single platform. DV360 is part of the Google Marketing Platform and offers advanced targeting, creative optimization, and cross-channel measurement capabilities.

What is the difference between DV360 and Google Ads?

DV360 is designed for enterprise advertisers with large budgets (₹10L+ monthly) and offers access to premium inventory across 90+ exchanges, advanced programmatic features, private marketplace deals, and CTV advertising. Google Ads targets SMBs with no minimum budget and focuses on Google's owned properties with emphasis on search advertising. DV360 offers deeper targeting, creative optimization, and measurement capabilities for brand awareness campaigns.

How much does Display & Video 360 cost?

DV360 operates on a percentage of media spend model, typically 10-15% platform fee on top of media costs. There's no setup fee for large advertisers, but minimum spend requirements typically start at ₹5-10 lakh monthly in India. Additional costs may include third-party data fees and creative production. Enterprise agreements can negotiate lower platform fees based on annual commitments.

Who should use DV360?

DV360 is ideal for enterprises spending ₹10L+ monthly on advertising, agencies managing multiple large accounts, brands running CTV and connected audio campaigns, advertisers needing private marketplace deals, and companies requiring advanced cross-channel measurement. It's not suitable for small businesses or those focused primarily on search advertising.

Does DV360 require certification?

While not strictly required, DV360 certification is recommended for effective platform use. Google offers Display & Video 360 certification through Skillshop. Many enterprises work with certified DV360 partners who have expertise in campaign setup, optimization, and measurement. Certification ensures understanding of programmatic buying, audience strategies, and platform features.
