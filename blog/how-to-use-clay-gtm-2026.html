What Is Clay 2026?

Clay is a data enrichment and workflow automation platform designed for go-to-market teams. Founded in 2017, Clay has grown rapidly and in 2026 is used by thousands of sales and marketing teams worldwide. Unlike traditional data providers that offer a single database, Clay aggregates data from 75+ providers and lets you build custom enrichment workflows in a spreadsheet-like interface.

Data Enrichment 2026

Enrichment is Clay's core functionality. Add data to your leads from multiple providers in 2026.

Available Data Providers 2026

Clay integrates with 75+ data providers including:

  • Contact data: Apollo, Hunter, Lusha, RocketReach, Clearbit
  • Company data: Clearbit, ZoomInfo, Crunchbase, BuiltWith
  • Email verification: ZeroBounce, NeverBounce, Debounce
  • Social data: LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub
  • Intent data: Bombora, G2

Basic Enrichment Workflow 2026

  1. Import your lead data (company domain, name, etc.)
  2. Add an enrichment column
  3. Select the data provider and field to enrich
  4. Run the enrichment
  5. Review and export results

Waterfall Enrichment 2026

Waterfall enrichment tries multiple data sources in sequence until data is found:

  • Set up primary, secondary, tertiary sources
  • Clay tries the first source
  • If no data found, automatically tries the next source
  • Continues until data is found or all sources exhausted

Waterfall Strategy Example 2026

Priority Provider Cost per Lookup Coverage
1st Hunter.io Low Medium
2nd Apollo Medium High
3rd Lusha Medium High
4th ZoomInfo High Very High

This approach minimizes cost while maximizing data coverage.

FAQs: Clay GTM 2026

What is Clay used for in 2026?

Clay is a data enrichment and automation platform used for go-to-market operations in 2026. It helps sales and marketing teams enrich leads with data from 75+ providers, build targeted prospect lists, score and qualify leads, personalize outreach at scale, and automate data workflows. Clay is popular for outbound sales, ABM, and revenue operations.

How much does Clay cost in 2026?

Clay pricing in 2026 is credit-based. Free plan includes 100 credits/month. Starter is $149/month for 2,000 credits. Explorer is $349/month for 10,000 credits. Pro is $800/month for 50,000 credits. Enterprise has custom pricing. Credits are consumed when enriching data or using AI features. Most active GTM teams need Explorer or Pro plans.

How is Clay different from ZoomInfo in 2026?

Clay differs from ZoomInfo in 2026 in several ways. ZoomInfo is a data provider with its own proprietary database. Clay is a data orchestration platform that aggregates data from 75+ providers including ZoomInfo. Clay offers more flexibility with waterfall enrichment (trying multiple sources), AI-powered features, and spreadsheet-like interface. ZoomInfo is better if you only need one data source.

Can Clay integrate with my CRM in 2026?

Yes, Clay integrates with major CRMs in 2026 including Salesforce, HubSpot, and Pipedrive. You can pull leads from your CRM into Clay for enrichment, then push enriched data back. Clay also integrates with outreach tools like Outreach, Apollo, and Instantly. Use webhooks or Zapier for additional integrations not natively supported.
