How Do You Get Your Products Into ChatGPT in 2026?

You share a structured product feed with OpenAI under the Agentic Commerce Protocol, after being accepted as an approved partner. OpenAI states that onboarding product feeds in ChatGPT is currently available to approved partners, with applications through chatgpt.com/merchants. Once accepted, you deliver product data by file upload, by API, or both, so ChatGPT can index your catalogue and show accurate titles, descriptions, images, price and availability in shopping experiences.

A feed is the foundation for three separate things, and each has its own switch: appearing in search and shopping answers, appearing in ChatGPT Ads, and being purchasable through checkout inside ChatGPT. Most coverage blurs these together. OpenAI's specification keeps them strictly apart, and so does this guide. It is the hub of a five-part series; the spokes cover field requirements, agentic checkout, promotions and the production launch checklist.

Explain It Like I Am Five

The simple version

Imagine a very helpful shop assistant who does not own a shop. People ask the assistant what to buy, and the assistant can only recommend things it has a list of. Your product feed is the list you hand over: what each thing is, what it costs, and whether it is on the shelf. A good list means the assistant can recommend you. A list with mistakes means it cannot. If you also build a till the assistant is allowed to use, people can pay without leaving the conversation, but the money still goes to your shop, and you still handle returns. And right now, only shops the assistant has invited can hand over a list at all.

What Is the Agentic Commerce Protocol in 2026?

The Agentic Commerce Protocol, or ACP, is the set of specifications OpenAI uses for commerce flows in ChatGPT, covering product feeds, agentic checkout and delegated payment. OpenAI points to agenticcommerce.dev and a public GitHub repository for the protocol itself, and publishes its implementation docs at developers.openai.com/commerce, including a machine-readable index at /commerce/llms.txt and Markdown versions of every page.

It is a different protocol from Google's Universal Commerce Protocol, which we covered in our UCP guide. For a brand, the strategic point is that AI shopping surfaces are converging on structured, machine-readable catalogue data with strict validation, whichever vendor is asking. Our broader guide to preparing a brand for AI shopping agents covers that strategy layer; this series covers OpenAI's actual specification.

Who Is Eligible to Use ChatGPT Product Feeds in 2026?

Approved partners, in markets OpenAI has confirmed for their integration, selling products that pass OpenAI's prohibited products policy. Each of those three conditions removes a meaningful share of brands, and it is better to know which applies to you before investing engineering time.

But a brand whose customers are mostly in India and pay mostly by UPI should treat ChatGPT checkout as a watch item rather than a 2026 launch, and should ask OpenAI directly about market availability before committing budget.

How Should You Deliver a Product Feed in 2026?

OpenAI's recommended pattern is the full feed once a day by file upload, then updates through the day by API. If your feed is small, you can provide both the full feed and regular updates via the API. File upload is delivered to an SFTP server, and for file upload you overwrite the same file or shard set with your latest snapshot on a regular cadence. For the API, you upsert products.

  1. Decide on file upload, API, or both.
  2. Review the specs for that method and confirm required fields, canonical field names and validation rules.
  3. Validate required fields for every record.
  4. Upload through the chosen method.
  5. Keep the feed current by overwriting snapshots or upserting through the API.

One exception changes the plan for most retailers: OpenAI states that promotions data can only be provided via the API. If discounts matter to your conversion, a file-only integration cannot carry them. Our promotions guide covers the schema and its three supported benefit types.

What Does a Product Feed Have to Contain in 2026?

Nine fields per row, one row per purchasable item or variant: item_id, title, description, url, brand, seller_name, image_url, availability and price. Two rules cause most rejected rows. Availability is a closed list, and omitted, empty or unrecognised values reject the row. Prices are written as an amount and a three-letter currency code, such as 79.99 USD, with no thousands separators.

The API product schema is structured differently, with products holding a list of variants and money expressed as integer minor units with a separate currency. Teams that use both delivery methods need two formatters, not one. The full field reference, including variants, returns, reviews and the Google-compatible feed path, is in our product feed requirements guide.

How Does Checkout Inside ChatGPT Work in 2026?

You host five HTTPS endpoints that ChatGPT calls to create, update, complete, cancel and retrieve a checkout session, and each response returns the full, authoritative cart. Payment runs through your existing PSP using a single-use delegated token, and you send signed webhooks when orders are created and updated. OpenAI is not the merchant of record: customers see your name on their card statement, and refunds and chargebacks stay with you.

Direct integration with the Delegated Payment Spec is only for PSPs or PCI DSS level 1 merchants using their own vaults; everyone else uses their PSP's implementation, with Stripe's Shared Payment Token named as the first compatible one. The endpoint-level detail is in our agentic checkout merchant guide, and the tests OpenAI requires before you go live are in our production checklist guide.

How Does the Feed Connect to ChatGPT Ads in 2026?

Through the same product data, with its own eligibility flag. In the OpenAI file format, is_ads_eligible set to true marks a product for Ads processing; omitted or empty means disabled unless a feed-level Ads default applies, and it is independent of search eligibility. Ads feeds follow OpenAI's Ads product feeds guide, and the optional ads_metadata field carries product-filter keys configured for your Ads integration, which OpenAI says you should confirm during Ads setup rather than invent.

For marketers this is the most immediately actionable part of the stack, because a feed that is already validated for discovery can be extended for Ads without a checkout build. Our ChatGPT Ads API guide covers campaign structure, bidding and the controls you actually have.

What Can You Not Control in ChatGPT Shopping in 2026?

Whether you are shown. OpenAI's specification says it in five words on the search eligibility flag: eligibility does not guarantee display. A valid, approved, fully populated feed makes your products available to be considered; it does not buy placement, and nothing in the commerce documentation describes a ranking you can configure. Paid placement is a separate product, ChatGPT Ads, with its own controls.

Several other things sit outside a merchant's control in the published specification, and it is better to plan around them than discover them.

How Do You Improve the Quality of Your ChatGPT Listings in 2026?

By writing for an answer engine rather than a product grid. OpenAI's best-practice guidance asks for concise, factual descriptions, accepting both plain text and bullet-style text. It notes that optional fields such as description.html, description.markdown, categories.taxonomy and seller.links can improve answer quality but are not required for ingestion, and it adds a sensible caution: if an optional field needs brittle transforms, omit it until the data is stable.

This is where the work overlaps with what Distk does for AI search visibility more broadly. A model choosing between products leans on specific, checkable attributes: material, dimensions, condition, return terms, review aggregates. Marketing copy that says "premium quality" gives it nothing to extract. A description that states what the product is made of, how big it is, and what happens if the customer returns it gives it everything. Variant-specific titles, URLs, images, availability and prices, which OpenAI asks for whenever those values differ by variant, matter for the same reason: an answer that recommends the right size in the wrong colour is worse than no answer.

  • State facts, not adjectives. Material, size, weight, compatibility and care instructions.
  • Declare condition explicitly. Omitted condition may be treated as new; used and refurbished must always be declared.
  • Supply returns data properly. Acceptance, window in days, and a policy URL, as separate fields.
  • Use consistent seller attribution. One seller name and durable public policy links across the catalogue.
  • Encode URLs correctly. OpenAI asks for valid, encoded URLs, for example %20 for spaces.

What Are the Common Mistakes With ChatGPT Product Feeds in 2026?

  • Building before applying. Feed onboarding is limited to approved partners.
  • Assuming your market is covered. Standard uploads target the US until OpenAI confirms otherwise.
  • Treating discovery, Ads and checkout as one switch. They are three separate eligibility controls.
  • Planning promotions into a file feed. They are API-only.
  • Exporting availability or price in the wrong format. Both reject rows.
  • Assuming OpenAI handles refunds. You remain merchant of record.
  • Skipping feed attribution. You will not be able to tell whether the channel works.
  • Selling prohibited categories. OpenAI may remove products or ban a seller.

Key Questions

Selling Through ChatGPT in 2026: FAQs

How do I get my products into ChatGPT in 2026?

Apply as a partner at chatgpt.com/merchants. OpenAI states feed onboarding is currently available to approved partners. Once accepted, you share a structured product feed by file upload, API or both, following the Agentic Commerce Protocol specification.

Can Indian brands sell to Indian customers through ChatGPT?

Not by default. OpenAI's feed specification states the standard upload currently targets the US and the shared format represents US, CA and MX, with other markets only after OpenAI confirms the integration. Brands should ask OpenAI about their market before committing budget.

Is appearing in ChatGPT the same as selling in ChatGPT?

No. Discovery, ChatGPT Ads and checkout are three separate capabilities with separate eligibility controls. Search defaults on, Ads defaults off, and checkout requires a separately enabled integration that implements the Agentic Checkout Spec.

Should I use file upload or the API for my feed?

OpenAI recommends the entire feed once a day by file upload and updates through the day by API. Small feeds can use the API for both. Promotions can only be provided via the API, so retailers running discounts need an API path regardless.

Does ChatGPT checkout support UPI?

Not in the current published specification. The checkout spec lists stripe, adyen and braintree as payment providers and card as the payment method.

Who handles refunds for orders placed in ChatGPT?

The merchant. OpenAI states the merchant selling the goods and taking payment is the merchant of record and handles refunds and chargebacks, syncing their status back to ChatGPT through the order update webhook.
