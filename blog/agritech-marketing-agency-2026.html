What Is Agritech Marketing in 2026?

Agritech marketing in 2026 is marketing for agriculture technology companies—farm management platforms, precision agriculture tools, agri-input marketplaces, commodity trading platforms, farm-to-fork solutions, and rural fintech. India's agritech sector requires understanding of farmer behavior, regional languages, and hybrid digital-physical distribution.

Agritech Marketing Strategy 2026

Digital Channels for Farmers 2026

Reaching farmers digitally:

  • WhatsApp groups and broadcasts
  • YouTube in regional languages
  • Facebook for farming communities
  • Voice and IVR campaigns
  • SMS and vernacular content

On-Ground Activations 2026

Building trust through presence:

  • Village-level demos and camps
  • Mandi and FPO partnerships
  • Agriculture fairs and events
  • Field officer networks
  • Kisan call centers

B2B Agribusiness Marketing 2026

Reaching agribusinesses:

  • LinkedIn for agri professionals
  • Industry events and conferences
  • Trade publications
  • Case studies with yield/ROI data
  • Partnership and channel marketing
