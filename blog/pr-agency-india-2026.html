Public Relations in 2026 is strategic communication that manages reputation and builds credibility through earned media. It encompasses media relations, thought leadership, content marketing, crisis communications, and stakeholder engagement—all increasingly integrated with digital marketing and SEO.

Thought Leadership 2026

Positioning founders and executives as experts:

  • Bylined articles in target publications
