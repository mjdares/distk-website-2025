What Is Affiliate Marketing in 2026?

Affiliate marketing in 2026 is a performance-based channel where external partners promote your products for commissions on sales or leads they generate. It includes content creators, review sites, comparison platforms, coupon sites, and influencers—all working on a pay-for-performance basis.
