What Is Bitrix24 2026?

Bitrix24 is a unified workspace platform that includes CRM, project management, communication tools, website builder, and marketing automation. Founded in 2012, it has grown to serve over 12 million organizations globally. In 2026, Bitrix24 offers both cloud and self-hosted deployment options.

Getting Started with Bitrix24 2026

Setting up Bitrix24 is straightforward. Follow these steps to get started in 2026:

Step 1: Create Your Account 2026

  1. Go to bitrix24.com and click "Get Started Free"
  2. Sign up with email or social login
  3. Choose your portal name (yourcompany.bitrix24.com)
  4. Complete the setup wizard

Step 2: Configure Basic Settings 2026

  • Company Profile: Add logo, company details, timezone
  • Users: Invite team members via email
  • Departments: Create organizational structure
  • Access Rights: Set permissions for different roles

Step 3: Set Up CRM 2026

  • Pipeline Stages: Customize deal stages for your sales process
  • Lead Sources: Define where leads come from
  • Custom Fields: Add fields specific to your business
  • Product Catalog: Add products or services you sell

Step 4: Connect Channels 2026

Bitrix24 can capture leads from multiple channels:

  • Website forms (CRM forms)
  • Email integration
  • Phone/telephony
  • Social media (Facebook, Instagram)
  • Messaging apps (WhatsApp, Telegram)
  • Live chat widget

CRM and Lead Management 2026

  1. Capture: Lead enters via form, call, or manual entry

  3. Convert: Transform qualified lead into contact + deal
  4. Nurture: Engage unconverted leads with content
  5. Close: Work deal through pipeline to won/lost

CRM Views and Filters 2026

Bitrix24 offers multiple ways to view CRM data:

  • Kanban: Visual board showing deals by stage
  • List: Traditional table view with sorting/filtering
  • Calendar: Time-based view of activities
  • Reports: Analytics and dashboards

Landing Pages 2026

  • Form integration for lead capture

  • Custom domain support

CRM Forms 2026

Capture leads directly into CRM:

  • Create forms with custom fields

  • Trigger automations on submission
  • Track form conversion rates

Marketing Segments 2026

Create targeted segments for campaigns:

  • Filter by any CRM field

  • Dynamic segments update automatically
  • Use for email, SMS, and ad targeting

Automation and Workflows 2026

Bitrix24 offers powerful automation through Robots and Triggers:

CRM Robots 2026

Robots automate actions within CRM stages:

  • Send Email: Automatic emails when deals reach stages
  • Create Task: Auto-assign follow-up tasks
  • Send Notification: Alert team members
  • Change Field: Update CRM fields automatically
  • Wait: Add delays between actions
  • Condition: Branch based on criteria

Setting Up Automation 2026

  1. Go to CRM > Settings > Automation Rules
  2. Select the pipeline to automate
  3. Choose stage where robot should run
  4. Add and configure robots
  5. Test with a sample record

Example Automations 2026

Trigger Actions Purpose
New lead created Send welcome email, create task Immediate follow-up
Deal reaches proposal stage Notify manager, schedule reminder Pipeline management
Lead inactive 7 days Send nurture email Re-engagement
Deal won Send thank you, create onboarding task Customer success

Triggers 2026

Triggers move deals between stages automatically based on conditions:

  • Email opened or clicked
  • Form submitted
  • Call completed
  • Website visited
  • Custom field changed

FAQs: Bitrix24 Marketing 2026

Is Bitrix24 free to use in 2026?

Bitrix24 offers a generous free plan in 2026 with unlimited users, 5GB storage, CRM, tasks, and basic collaboration tools. Paid plans (Basic, Standard, Professional, Enterprise) unlock more storage, advanced automation, marketing features, and telephony. The free plan is suitable for small teams getting started.

How does Bitrix24 compare to HubSpot in 2026?

Bitrix24 offers more features in its free tier than HubSpot, including unlimited users and built-in collaboration tools (tasks, chat, video calls). HubSpot has stronger marketing automation and a more polished interface. Bitrix24 is better for teams needing an all-in-one workspace. HubSpot is better for dedicated marketing automation.

Can Bitrix24 be self-hosted in 2026?

Yes, Bitrix24 offers both cloud and self-hosted (on-premise) versions in 2026. Self-hosted requires purchasing a license and managing your own servers. It provides full data control and customization options. Most small to mid-sized businesses use the cloud version for easier maintenance.

What marketing features does Bitrix24 offer in 2026?

Bitrix24 marketing features in 2026 include: CRM with lead and deal management, email marketing with templates, landing page builder, web forms, sales automation, basic marketing automation, social media integration, and telephony. Advanced marketing automation requires Professional or Enterprise plans.
