An event marketing agency in 2026 creates experiences that generate leads, build relationships, and drive revenue—from webinars and virtual conferences to hybrid experiences and live events. Events remain one of the most effective channels for B2B lead generation and customer engagement.

What Is Event Marketing in 2026?

Event marketing in 2026 uses gatherings—virtual, hybrid, or in-person—as marketing channels. It encompasses webinars, virtual conferences, trade shows, workshops, customer events, and exclusive experiences, all designed with specific business objectives and measurable outcomes.

Event Marketing Strategy 2026

Event Planning 2026

Setting up for success:

  • Objective and KPI definition
  • Target audience and positioning
  • Format and platform selection
  • Content and speaker strategy
  • Budget and timeline planning

Event Promotion 2026

Driving registrations:

  • Email campaigns to existing lists
  • Social media promotion
  • Paid advertising for reach
  • Partner and sponsor promotion
  • Speaker network amplification

Event Execution 2026

Delivering great experiences:

  • Platform setup and testing
  • Speaker preparation and run-of-show
  • Live production and moderation
  • Attendee engagement tools
  • Real-time troubleshooting

Post-Event 2026

Converting attendees to pipeline:

  • On-demand content availability
  • Attendee follow-up sequences
  • Lead scoring and sales handoff
  • ROI analysis and reporting
  • Content repurposing
