What Is Podcast Marketing in 2026?

Podcast marketing in 2026 encompasses two main approaches: creating original branded podcasts as owned media, and advertising on existing podcasts to reach established audiences. Both leverage the intimate, trusted nature of podcast listening for brand building and lead generation.

Branded Podcast Strategy 2026

Podcast Development 2026

Creating a successful branded podcast:

  • Concept development and positioning
  • Format selection (interview, solo, panel, narrative)
  • Target audience alignment
  • Episode planning and content calendar
  • Host selection and training

Production 2026

Delivering professional quality:

  • Recording setup and remote recording coordination
  • Audio editing and mixing
  • Show notes and transcripts
  • Cover art and episode graphics
  • Distribution to all platforms

Promotion & Growth 2026

Building audience:

  • Launch strategy and initial promotion
  • Social media clips and audiograms
  • Cross-promotion with other podcasts
  • Paid promotion on podcast platforms
  • Email marketing integration
