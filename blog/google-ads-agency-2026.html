Performance Max campaigns in 2026 dominate Google advertising. Success requires:

  • Search Theme Exclusions: Brand terms in separate Search campaigns

FAQs: Google Ads Agency 2026

What is Performance Max in Google Ads 2026?

Performance Max (PMax) in 2026 is Google's AI-powered campaign type that automatically serves ads across Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Maps, using machine learning to optimize based on conversion goals.

How much do Google ads cost in India 2026?

Google ads costs in India 2026 vary: Search CPC ₹20-500+, Display CPM ₹30-100, YouTube CPV ₹1-5. Agency fees range ₹50,000-3,00,000/month.

Should I use Search or Performance Max in 2026?

Use both: Search for high-intent keywords with precise control, Performance Max for broad reach and AI optimization. PMax finds new audiences while Search captures bottom-funnel demand.
