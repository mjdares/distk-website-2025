What Is Perplexity 2026?

Perplexity is an AI-powered answer engine that combines large language models with real-time web search. Instead of returning a list of links like Google, Perplexity reads multiple sources, synthesizes the information, and delivers a comprehensive answer with numbered citations you can verify.

Perplexity Key Features 2026

  • Real-time search: Accesses current web information, not outdated training data
  • Source citations: Every claim includes numbered references you can check
  • Follow-up questions: Refine your research with contextual follow-ups
  • Multiple AI models: Access GPT-4, Claude, and Perplexity's own models
  • Focus modes: Target specific source types (academic, Reddit, YouTube, etc.)
  • Collections: Organize research into shareable folders
  • File analysis: Upload documents for AI analysis (Pro)

Competitive Intelligence 2026

Competitor Overview Query 2026

"Give me a comprehensive overview of [Competitor Name]. Include: company founding and funding, main products/services, target audience, pricing model, recent news or announcements, and key differentiators. Focus on information from 2025-2026."

Competitive Positioning Query 2026

"Compare [Your Company Type] competitors: [Competitor 1], [Competitor 2], [Competitor 3]. Create a comparison of: target market, pricing, key features, strengths, and weaknesses. Include recent reviews or customer feedback."

Competitor Marketing Analysis 2026

"Analyze [Competitor Name]'s marketing strategy. Find information about: their main marketing channels, content marketing approach, social media presence, recent campaigns or ads, and messaging/positioning. Include any recent changes in 2025-2026."

Competitor Technology Stack 2026

"What technology and tools does [Competitor Name] use? Look for information about their: website technology, marketing automation tools, analytics platforms, CRM, and any recent technology changes or integrations they've announced."

Pricing Intelligence Query 2026

"Research pricing for [product category] software. Compare pricing from top vendors including [list competitors]. Include: pricing tiers, features at each tier, per-user vs flat pricing, and any recent pricing changes. Focus on current 2026 pricing."

Market Research 2026

Market Size Query 2026

"What is the current market size for [industry/product category] in [region]? Include: total market value, projected growth rate (CAGR), key growth drivers, and major market segments. Cite recent market research reports from 2025-2026."

Industry Trends Query 2026

"What are the top trends shaping the [industry] in 2026? List at least 7 trends with: brief description, impact on businesses, examples of companies leading each trend, and supporting data or statistics."

Target Audience Research 2026

"Research the typical buyer profile for [product/service type]. Find information about: demographics, job titles and roles, common pain points, buying decision factors, preferred information sources, and typical budget ranges."

Regional Market Analysis 2026

"Analyze the [industry] market in India for 2026. Include: market size, key players, growth opportunities, regulatory considerations, consumer behavior trends, and challenges for new entrants."

Industry Statistics Query 2026

"Find the latest statistics for [industry/topic]. I need data on: [specific metric 1], [specific metric 2], [specific metric 3]. Only include statistics from 2024-2026 with clear sources."

Content Research 2026

Content Topic Research 2026

"What are the most searched questions and topics about [subject] in 2026? Find: common questions people ask, trending subtopics, pain points being discussed, and content gaps that aren't well-covered. Check Reddit, Quora, and industry forums."

Keyword Intent Research 2026

"Research the search intent behind '[keyword]'. What are people actually looking for when they search this? Include: types of content that rank well, common related searches, user problems being solved, and content format preferences."

Content Gap Analysis 2026

"Analyze the top content ranking for '[keyword]'. What topics do they cover? What's missing? Identify: common sections across top results, unique angles some take, topics not well covered, and opportunities for more comprehensive content."

Expert Sources Query 2026

"Find experts and thought leaders in [topic/industry] who I could cite or reference in content. Look for: academics, practitioners, authors, active commentators. Include their credentials and notable opinions or quotes on [specific aspect]."

Statistics for Content 2026

"Find compelling statistics about [topic] for a blog post. I need 10-15 recent statistics (2024-2026) that would support content about [angle/argument]. Include the source for each statistic."

Trend Analysis 2026

Emerging Trends Query 2026

"What emerging trends in [industry] should marketers prepare for in 2026-2027? Focus on: new technologies, changing consumer behaviors, regulatory changes, and market shifts. Include early signals and examples."

Technology Trends Query 2026

"How is [technology - e.g., AI, AR, blockchain] being used in [industry] marketing in 2026? Find: current applications, companies leading adoption, results being achieved, and predictions for growth."

Consumer Behavior Trends 2026

"How are consumer behaviors changing in [industry/category] in 2026? Research: purchasing patterns, channel preferences, decision-making factors, generational differences, and impact of economic conditions."

Social Media Trends 2026

"What are the latest [platform - LinkedIn/Instagram/TikTok] trends for B2B/B2C marketing in 2026? Include: content formats performing well, algorithm changes, engagement strategies, and successful campaign examples."

Best Practices 2026

Writing Effective Queries 2026

  • Be specific: Include exactly what you need
  • Set time frames: Specify "2025-2026" for current data
  • Request structure: Ask for bullet points, tables, comparisons
  • Use follow-ups: Refine results with follow-up questions
  • Specify sources: Request academic, industry, or news sources

Verifying Information 2026

  • Check citations: Click through to verify important claims
  • Cross-reference: Ask the same question differently
  • Note dates: Ensure statistics are current
  • Primary sources: Request original sources when possible

Organizing Research 2026

  • Create collections by project or topic
  • Save important searches for reference
  • Export findings to documents
  • Share collections with stakeholders
  • Build a research library over time

Combining with Other Tools 2026

What is Perplexity used for in marketing 2026?
