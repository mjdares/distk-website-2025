What Is Luxury Marketing in 2026?

Luxury marketing in 2026 is specialized marketing for premium and luxury brands. Unlike mass-market marketing, it emphasizes exclusivity, heritage, craftsmanship, and experience over price or convenience. India's rapidly growing HNI and UHNI population makes luxury marketing increasingly important.
