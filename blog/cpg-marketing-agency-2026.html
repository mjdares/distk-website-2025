CPG Marketing Agency India 2026: Complete Guide to FMCG, Consumer Goods & Retail Marketing

January 202614 min read

A CPG marketing agency in 2026 builds consumer goods brands and drives sales through integrated marketing—combining mass media, digital, retail media, shopper marketing, and D2C strategies for FMCG products. CPG marketing requires balancing brand building with short-term sales activation.

What Is CPG Marketing in 2026?

CPG (Consumer Packaged Goods) marketing in 2026 is marketing for everyday products sold in retail—food, beverages, personal care, household goods, and packaged commodities. It combines long-term brand building with short-term sales activation across traditional, digital, and retail channels.

CPG Marketing Strategy 2026

Brand Building 2026

Creating long-term equity:

  • TV and video advertising
  • Brand purpose and positioning
  • Sponsorships and associations
  • Packaging and visual identity
  • Innovation and NPD launches

Digital Marketing 2026

Targeted reach and engagement:

  • Social media and content
  • Influencer partnerships
  • Programmatic display
  • YouTube pre-roll
  • D2C website and conversion

Shopper Marketing 2026

Converting at point of purchase:

  • Retail media (Amazon, Flipkart)
  • In-store displays and promotions
  • Sampling and trial programs
  • Trade promotion optimization
  • E-commerce merchandising
