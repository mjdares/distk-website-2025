Growth Hacking Framework 2026

The Growth Loop Model 2026

Growth loops are self-reinforcing systems that compound:

  • Viral Loop: User → Invites friends → New users → More invites
  • Content Loop: Content → Traffic → Signups → User content → More traffic
  • Paid Loop: Revenue → Reinvest in ads → More users → More revenue
  • Sales Loop: Customer → Case study → Credibility → More customers

Experimentation Process 2026

Systematic approach to growth experiments:

  • Ideation: Generate hypotheses from data and observation
  • Prioritization: ICE score (Impact × Confidence × Ease)
  • Testing: Run minimum viable experiments quickly
  • Analysis: Measure results with statistical rigor
  • Scale: Double down on winners, kill losers fast

Viral Mechanics 2026

Building virality into products:

  • Incentivized Sharing: Rewards for inviting friends
  • Social Proof: Public activity, leaderboards, badges
  • Collaborative Features: Invite to collaborate
  • Content Sharing: Easy sharing of user creations
  • Network Effects: Product improves with more users
