Best Practices for WhatsApp Marketing with Wati 2026

Build Your Bot Before Broadcasting 2026

Set up your chatbot flows, team inbox, and automation workflows before launching broadcast campaigns. When thousands of customers respond to your first campaign, you need automation and agents ready to handle the volume. A broadcast without a response system is wasted spend.

Segment Aggressively 2026

WhatsApp marketing with Wati works best when every message feels personal. Segment by purchase history, engagement level, geography, language, and custom attributes. Use Wati's tag system to build dynamic segments that update automatically as customer behaviour changes in 2026.

Deploy AI Agents for Scale 2026

Use Astra for general inbound engagement and Knowbot for detailed product and policy queries. AI agents handle 60% of queries without human intervention in 2026 — letting your team focus on high-value conversations like closing sales and handling complex complaints.

Respect the Channel 2026

WhatsApp is personal. Over-messaging will destroy your opt-in list faster than any other channel. Two to four campaigns per week maximum. Every message must deliver value — a genuine offer, useful information, or a relevant update. Never spam. Monitor your opt-out rate and keep it below 2% per campaign.

Track the Right Metrics 2026

  • Delivery rate: Should be 95%+ (if lower, clean your contact list)
  • Read rate: 98% is the WhatsApp benchmark
  • Reply rate: 15-25% for well-segmented campaigns
  • Bot deflection rate: Target 60%+ with Astra and Knowbot
  • Cart recovery rate: 15-25% through Shopify integration
  • Opt-out rate: Keep below 2% — higher means you are over-messaging
