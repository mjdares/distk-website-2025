What Is Education Marketing in 2026?

Education marketing in 2026 encompasses digital marketing for all educational entities—from K-12 schools and coaching centers to universities and online learning platforms. It addresses the unique challenges of long decision cycles, multiple stakeholders (students + parents), and seasonal admission patterns.
