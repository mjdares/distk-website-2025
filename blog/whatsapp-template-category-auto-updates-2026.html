Why an Approved WhatsApp Template Can Still Be Reclassified in 2026

A template approved as utility can be billed as marketing later. Meta confirmed in September 2026 that approval does not exempt a template from automatic category updates, and its pricing rules say you pay the category in force when you send. Here is how to see it coming and what to do.

Can an Approved WhatsApp Template Still Be Recategorised in 2026?

Yes. On 15 September 2026 Meta clarified that a template whose category was confirmed by a review remains subject to automatic category updates. If the messages the template sends do not meet the template category guidelines, action can still be taken on the template. Approval is a starting point, not a guarantee that the category, and therefore the price, stays fixed.

This is the single most common billing surprise on the WhatsApp Business Platform. A team builds a flow around a template approved as utility, budgets it at the utility rate, and later finds it billed as marketing. Meta's pricing documentation is explicit about whose problem that is: businesses are responsible for reviewing the category assigned to their approved templates, and whenever a template is used, the business accepts the charges for the category applied to it at the time of use.

The sentence that settles every dispute in 2026

"Whenever a template is used, a business accepts the charges associated with the category applied to the template at time of use." The category that matters is the one in force when you send, not the one you were approved with. If it changed between the two, you pay the new rate.

Why Does a Category Change Matter So Much in 2026?

Because the category decides the rate. WhatsApp templates are categorised as marketing, utility or authentication, and each carries its own per-message price that varies by market. Marketing templates are always charged. Utility templates have different treatment depending on whether they are sent inside or outside the 24-hour customer service window. A move between categories can change both whether a message is charged and what it costs.

Meta's own worked example shows how the categories behave across a single customer journey. It is reproduced below as published, with one important caveat about the 1 October 2026 change that follows it.

Meta begins charging for utility messages sent in response to users inside the 24-hour customer service window, which had not been charged since 1 July 2025. Service messages also become chargeable beyond a free tier on the same date. Our service message pricing hub covers both changes in full. The point for this guide is simpler: from October, more of a template's life is billable, so its category matters on more of your sends.

How Does WhatsApp Tell You a Category Is Changing in 2026?

Through the template_category_update webhook. It fires in two situations: when a template's existing category is going to be changed by an automated process, and when a template's existing category has been changed, manually or by an automated process. The first is your warning. The second is your confirmation.

The field-name trap in 2026

In the impending notification, new_category holds the template's current category and correct_category holds the one it is about to receive. Read the names at face value and you will log the change backwards. Map correct_category to "what it is becoming" and category_update_timestamp to "when", and treat new_category as "what it is now" until the completed notification arrives.

The impending notification is the useful one. Meta's reference describes roughly a day of notice before an automated recategorisation, with category_update_timestamp giving the exact moment the change takes effect. That window is your chance to pause a high-volume send, edit the template, or move the content to a correctly categorised template before the new rate applies.

Why Do Templates Get Recategorised After Approval in 2026?

Because review checks a template at one moment, and Meta's automated process keeps checking what the template actually sends. Meta's clarification ties action to whether the messages a template sends meet the category guidelines. A template approved as utility that, in practice, carries promotional content is the textbook case. The approval reflected what it looked like at review. The recategorisation reflects how it is being used.

  • Promotion inside a transactional template: an order confirmation that also pushes a discount code is using a utility template for marketing.
  • Variables that change the meaning: a template whose variable fields carry promotional content at send time can drift from its approved purpose.
  • One template doing several jobs: a general-purpose template reused across campaigns is harder to keep inside one category's guidelines.

The practical rule follows directly. Keep each template to one job, and keep promotional content in marketing templates, so that what a template sends always matches what it was approved as.

How Should You Handle a Category Change When It Arrives in 2026?

Meta added template message routing guidance on 24 September 2026, covering how to maintain an up-to-date template-category mapping, how to select the correct send endpoint, and how to recover from category changes. The operational version is a short loop that should run automatically rather than in a spreadsheet.

  1. Subscribe to template_category_update and store every notification.
  2. Keep your own template-to-category map and update it from the webhook, never from memory.
  3. Alert a human on every impending notification for any template with meaningful volume.
  4. Decide before category_update_timestamp: accept the new rate, edit the template, or switch to a template already in the right category.
  5. Re-cost affected flows at the new category's rate for each market you send to.
  6. Route sends using the current category, since Meta's routing guidance ties the send endpoint to the category.
  7. Confirm with the completed notification, using previous_category and new_category.

A note on moving content to a new template

Creating a fresh template is not free of cost either. Under the new business account model, templates migrated to a new Messaging account reset to baseline quality rating and messaging tier and need roughly two to four weeks to ramp up. A new template starts without history. If a high-volume utility flow is recategorised, the fastest fix is often to remove the promotional content from the existing template rather than to replace it.

How Should Marketing Teams Prevent Category Surprises in 2026?

Treat category as a budget input owned by marketing, not a technical detail owned by whoever submitted the template. The team writing the copy is the team most likely to push a utility template over the line.

What Are the Common Mistakes in 2026?

  • Treating approval as permanent. Meta confirmed on 15 September 2026 that approved templates remain subject to automatic category updates.
  • Budgeting at the approved category's rate. You are charged for the category in force at time of use.
  • Reading new_category as the incoming category. In the impending notification it is the current one; correct_category is the incoming one.
  • Ignoring the impending notification. It is the only window to act before the new rate applies.
  • Adding offers to utility templates. The most common cause of recategorisation.
  • Replacing a template reflexively. A new template starts without quality history.
  • Forgetting the October change. Utility templates inside the customer service window become chargeable from 1 October 2026.

Can WhatsApp change a template's category after it is approved?

Yes. Meta clarified on 15 September 2026 that a template whose category was confirmed by a review remains subject to automatic category updates. If the messages the template sends do not meet the category guidelines, action can still be taken on it.

Which category am I charged for if my template is recategorised?

The category applied to the template at the time of use. Meta's pricing documentation states that whenever a template is used, the business accepts the charges associated with the category applied at that time, and that businesses are responsible for reviewing their templates' categories.

How do I find out my WhatsApp template category is changing?

Subscribe to the template_category_update webhook. It sends an impending notification before an automated process changes a template's category, and a completed notification after a change made manually or automatically.

What do correct_category and new_category mean in the webhook?

Should I create a new template when one is recategorised?
