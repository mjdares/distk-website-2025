Zoho Books Plus vs Standard 2026

Zoho Books is the finance and accounting module available as a standalone product or as part of Zoho ERP. Understanding the difference between standard Zoho Books and Zoho Books Plus helps Indian businesses choose the right configuration in 2026.

Standalone accounting software focused on core financial management:

  • Invoicing & Billing: Create and send professional GST-compliant invoices
  • Expense Tracking: Record and categorize business expenses
  • Banking: Reconciliation and bank feed integration
  • Basic Inventory: Simple item tracking for trading businesses
  • GST Filing: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B filing and e-way bill generation
  • Reports: Standard financial reports (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow)

Getting Started with Zoho ERP 2026

Step 1: Sign Up and Organization Setup 2026

  1. Visit zoho.com/en-in/erp/ and click "Get Started"
  2. Create Zoho account using business email (avoid Gmail for business accounts)
  3. Choose organization name and industry vertical
  4. Select accounting standard (Indian GAAP for most Indian businesses)
  5. Set fiscal year (April-March for most Indian companies)
  6. Configure currency (INR as base, add foreign currencies if exporting)

Step 2: GST Configuration for Indian Businesses 2026

  1. Navigate to Settings → Taxes → GST Settings
  2. Enter GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number)
  3. Configure tax rates: Select applicable GST rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%)
  4. Set up HSN/SAC codes for your products and services
  5. Configure place of supply rules (state-wise for SGST/CGST vs IGST)
  6. Enable e-invoicing if annual turnover exceeds INR 10 crore
  7. Connect to GSTN portal with username and password for direct filing

Step 3: Chart of Accounts Customization 2026

  1. Start with Zoho's default Indian chart of accounts template
  2. Add custom accounts for your specific business (e.g., security deposits, TDS receivable)
  3. Create sub-accounts for detailed tracking (e.g., separate accounts for rent by location)
  4. Set up bank accounts and credit card accounts for reconciliation
  5. Configure opening balances if migrating from another system

Step 4: Master Data Import 2026

  • Customers: Import customer list with GSTIN, billing addresses, and payment terms
  • Vendors: Upload supplier information with GSTIN and procurement terms
  • Items: Import product/service catalog with HSN/SAC codes and tax rates
  • Opening Balances: Enter opening stock values and outstanding invoices
  • Employees: Import employee database if using Zoho People integration

Step 5: Integration Setup 2026

  • Banking: Connect Indian bank accounts through ICICI, HDFC, Axis Bank integrations
  • Payment Gateway: Integrate Razorpay, PayU, or Instamojo for online payments
  • E-commerce: Connect Shopify, WooCommerce, or marketplace accounts
  • Shipping: Set up BlueDart, DTDC, or Delhivery for automated shipping
  • Zoho Apps: Activate Zoho CRM, Desk, and Campaigns for full ecosystem benefits

Step 6: User Access and Permissions 2026

  1. Create user accounts for team members (accountant, sales, operations, management)
  2. Assign roles: Define permissions based on job function (view only, create, edit, delete)
  3. Set up approval workflows: Configure multi-level approvals for purchases and expenses
  4. Enable two-factor authentication for security compliance
  5. Configure email notifications for important events (invoice due, low stock, approvals)

GST-Compliant Invoicing 2026

Zoho ERP automatically generates GST-compliant invoices for Indian businesses:

  • HSN/SAC Codes: Pre-populated based on item configuration with search functionality

  • Invoice Format: Meets all GST requirements including GSTIN, reverse charge mention, and place of supply
  • Multi-language: Generate invoices in English and regional Indian languages

GST Return Filing Workflow 2026

GSTR-1 (Outward Supplies):

  1. Zoho Books automatically compiles all sales invoices for the tax period
  2. Review sales summary: B2B invoices, B2C supplies, exports, nil-rated, and exempted sales
  3. Verify invoice-level details and make corrections if needed
  4. Click "File GSTR-1" to submit directly to GSTN portal through API integration
  5. Download filed return JSON for records

GSTR-3B (Summary Return):

  1. System auto-populates outward supplies from GSTR-1
  2. Enter inward supplies (purchases) if not using purchase module
  3. Review input tax credit (ITC) available and utilized
  4. Enter tax liability, ITC claimed, and calculate net tax payable
  5. File through Zoho Books portal integration and generate challan for payment

GSTR-9 (Annual Return):

  • Zoho Books compiles year-long transaction data automatically
  • Consolidated view of outward supplies, inward supplies, and ITC across all months
  • Reconciliation report between books and GST returns
  • One-click filing after CA/tax consultant review

E-way Bill Generation 2026

For goods movement exceeding INR 50,000 value:

  1. Create invoice in Zoho Books with transporter details
  2. Click "Generate E-way Bill" button on invoice screen
  3. System auto-fills details from invoice: GSTIN, items, HSN, value
  4. Enter vehicle number and transport document details
  5. Submit to NIC e-way bill portal through API
  6. E-way bill number reflected on invoice automatically

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) Management 2026

  • TDS on Payments: Configure TDS rates for vendor payments (Section 194C, 194J, etc.)
  • Automatic Calculation: System calculates TDS when recording payments to vendors
  • TDS Returns: Generate Form 26Q (quarterly TDS return) from payment data
  • Form 16A: Issue TDS certificates to vendors automatically
  • TDS Receivable: Track TDS deducted by customers on your invoices

Bank Reconciliation 2026

Zoho ERP supports both manual and automated bank reconciliation:

  • Bank Feeds: Direct integration with ICICI, HDFC, Axis, and other Indian banks
  • Statement Upload: Import bank statements in Excel or CSV format
  • Auto-matching: System suggests matches between bank transactions and book entries
  • Bulk Reconciliation: Match multiple transactions simultaneously
  • Reconciliation Reports: View matched vs unmatched transactions with drill-down

Multi-currency for Export Businesses 2026

  • Foreign Currency Invoices: Bill international customers in USD, EUR, GBP, etc.
  • Exchange Rate Management: Automatic rate updates or manual rate entry per transaction
  • Currency Revaluation: Mark-to-market revaluation of foreign currency balances
  • Gain/Loss Accounting: Automatic booking of forex gains and losses
  • Export Documentation: Generate shipping bills and export invoices

Zoho ERP supports multiple inventory valuation methods for Indian accounting:

  • Batch/Lot Costing: Track cost by specific batch for pharmaceuticals and FMCG

Inventory Management Workflows 2026

Purchase to Stock:

  1. Create purchase order when stock reaches reorder level
  2. Send PO to vendor via email directly from Zoho
  3. Record goods receipt when shipment arrives
  4. Inspect quality and accept/reject items
  5. Generate vendor bill linked to purchase order
  6. Make payment with automatic TDS calculation

Sale to Dispatch:

  1. Receive sales order from customer or sales team
  2. Check stock availability across warehouses
  3. Create shipment order with warehouse allocation
  4. Generate packing slip for warehouse team
  5. Create GST invoice linked to sales order
  6. Generate e-way bill if required (value > INR 50,000)
  7. Book courier and track shipment status
  8. Record payment when customer pays

Reorder Automation 2026

  • Reorder Point: Set minimum stock level that triggers reorder alert
  • Reorder Quantity: Define economic order quantity for optimal purchasing
  • Preferred Vendor: Auto-select vendor based on item configuration
  • Auto PO Generation: System creates draft purchase orders when stock is low
  • Seasonal Adjustments: Modify reorder points based on seasonal demand patterns

FAQs: Zoho ERP India 2026

What is Zoho ERP used for?

Zoho ERP is a cloud-based enterprise resource planning platform used by Indian businesses to manage core operations including finance and accounting, inventory and supply chain, procurement, billing and subscriptions, payroll and HR, CRM and sales, manufacturing, and commerce. It provides end-to-end business management from order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, and hire-to-retire workflows with built-in GST compliance for Indian businesses.

Zoho Books vs Zoho Books Plus difference?

Zoho Books is the standalone accounting and finance module, while Zoho Books Plus refers to the enhanced version within Zoho ERP that integrates deeper with inventory management, procurement, manufacturing, and other operational modules. Books Plus includes advanced features like multi-warehouse management, production planning, advanced procurement workflows, and tighter integration with the complete Zoho ecosystem including CRM, Desk, and People.

Is Zoho ERP suitable for Indian businesses?

Yes, Zoho ERP is specifically designed for Indian businesses with built-in GST compliance including GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-9 filing, automatic tax calculation for CGST/SGST/IGST, HSN/SAC code support, e-way bill generation, and e-invoicing integration. Zoho launched its ERP platform in January 2026 targeting India's underserved SME market with affordable pricing starting from INR 899/month, making it accessible for startups to enterprises.

How much does Zoho ERP cost in India?

Zoho ERP pricing in India 2026 starts from INR 899/month for the basic plan allowing 3 users. Zoho Books standard pricing ranges from INR 899 to INR 18,000/month depending on features and users. For complete Zoho One (entire ecosystem), pricing is INR 1,500/employee/month billed annually, excluding 18% GST. Zoho offers free trials and free versions for solopreneurs and micro businesses, with transparent pricing and no hidden implementation costs.

Does Zoho ERP support GST compliance?

Yes, Zoho ERP has comprehensive GST compliance built-in for Indian businesses. It automatically detects and applies correct CGST, SGST, or IGST to transactions, supports HSN/SAC code classification, generates GST-compliant invoices, enables direct filing of GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, and GSTR-9 through GSTN portal integration, creates e-way bills automatically, and supports e-invoicing requirements. All GST features are available across standard plans without additional costs.
