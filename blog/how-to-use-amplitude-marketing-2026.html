What Is Amplitude 2026?

Amplitude is a digital analytics platform that helps teams understand user behavior through event-based tracking, advanced analytics, and experimentation. Unlike traditional web analytics, Amplitude focuses on product analytics—understanding what users do inside your product and why. In 2026, it offers a complete suite including Analytics, Experiment, CDP, and Session Replay.

Running Experiments 2026

  1. Define hypothesis: What do you expect and why?
  2. Choose metrics: Primary, secondary, guardrail
  3. Calculate sample size: Ensure statistical power
  4. Set up variants: Control and treatment
  5. Run to completion: Don't peek and stop early
  6. Analyze results: Check significance and segments
  7. Document learnings: Win, lose, or inconclusive

Experiment Best Practices 2026

  • One change per test: Isolate variables
  • Guardrail metrics: Ensure no negative impact
  • Segment analysis: Results may vary by group
  • Run long enough: Account for weekly patterns
  • Document everything: Build organizational knowledge

FAQs: Amplitude Marketing 2026

What is Amplitude used for in marketing 2026?

Amplitude is used by marketing teams in 2026 for product analytics and behavioral insights. Marketers use Amplitude to understand user journeys, analyze conversion funnels, run A/B experiments, and measure feature adoption. This data helps optimize acquisition strategies, improve onboarding, and increase retention through data-driven decisions.

How much does Amplitude cost for marketing teams in 2026?

Amplitude offers tiered pricing in 2026. The Starter plan is free with up to 50K monthly tracked users. Growth plan pricing is usage-based starting around $61/month. Enterprise adds advanced features like SSO, custom roles, and dedicated support. Most marketing teams start with Growth for full experimentation features.

How does Amplitude compare to Mixpanel in 2026?

Amplitude and Mixpanel are both event-based analytics platforms in 2026, but they differ in focus. Amplitude emphasizes self-serve analytics, experimentation, and AI-powered insights. Mixpanel focuses on ease of use and quick implementation. Amplitude's Experiment product is more mature, while Mixpanel offers simpler onboarding. Choice depends on team size and analytics sophistication.

What marketing experiments can you run with Amplitude in 2026?

Amplitude Experiment supports A/B tests, multivariate tests, and feature flags for marketing in 2026. Test landing page variants, onboarding flows, pricing pages, CTA designs, email triggers, and feature rollouts. The platform provides statistical significance calculations, segment analysis, and automatic winner detection for confident decision-making.
