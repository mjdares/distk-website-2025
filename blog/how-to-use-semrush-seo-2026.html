What Is Semrush 2026?

Semrush is a comprehensive digital marketing platform founded in 2008. In 2026, it has grown into one of the most feature-rich marketing tools available, with 55+ tools covering SEO, advertising, content, social media, and competitive research. The platform maintains databases covering 26 billion keywords and 43 trillion backlinks.

Getting Started with Semrush 2026

Setting up Semrush properly ensures you get maximum value from the platform. Follow these steps for a proper setup in 2026:

Step 1: Create Your Account 2026

  1. Go to semrush.com and create an account
  2. Start with a free trial or select a plan
  3. Complete account verification
  4. Access your dashboard

Step 2: Set Up Your Project 2026

  • Add Your Domain: Create a project for your main website
  • Connect Google Analytics: Link GA4 for enhanced traffic data
  • Connect Search Console: Import GSC data for accurate keyword tracking
  • Set Location: Choose your target country and city
  • Add Competitors: Input 3-5 main competitors to track

Step 3: Configure Site Audit 2026

  1. Go to Site Audit in the left menu
  2. Configure crawl settings (page limit, crawl source)
  3. Set allowed/disallowed URLs if needed
  4. Schedule weekly automatic crawls
  5. Run your first audit

Step 4: Set Up Position Tracking 2026

  • Add target keywords to monitor
  • Select device (desktop/mobile) and location
  • Add competitors to compare rankings
  • Enable email alerts for ranking changes

Keyword Research 2026

Semrush offers multiple keyword research tools with unique data including search intent classification. Here is how to use them in 2026:

Keyword Magic Tool 2026

The primary keyword research tool in Semrush:

  1. Enter a seed keyword
  2. Select your target country
  3. Browse keyword groups in the left sidebar
  4. Filter by volume, KD%, intent, and more
  5. Export keywords or add to Keyword Manager

Key Metrics in Semrush 2026

Metric What It Means How to Use It
Volume Average monthly searches Prioritize higher volume, consider seasonality
Keyword Difficulty (KD%) Competition level (0-100) Target under 40% for new sites
Intent Informational, Commercial, Transactional, Navigational Match content type to intent
CPC Cost per click in ads Higher CPC = higher commercial value
Trend 12-month search trend Identify growing or declining topics

Search Intent Categories 2026

Semrush classifies every keyword by intent:

  • Informational (I): User wants to learn something - "what is SEO"
  • Commercial (C): User is researching before purchase - "best SEO tools"
  • Transactional (T): User wants to take action - "buy Semrush subscription"
  • Navigational (N): User looking for specific site - "Semrush login"

Keyword Gap Analysis 2026

Find keywords your competitors rank for that you do not:

  1. Go to Keyword Gap tool
  2. Enter your domain and up to 4 competitors
  3. Review the overlap visualization
  4. Filter for "Missing" or "Weak" keywords
  5. Export opportunities and plan content

Competitive Analysis 2026

Competitive research is one of Semrush's strongest capabilities in 2026. Understanding competitor strategies helps inform your own.

Domain Overview 2026

Get a complete picture of any domain:

  1. Enter a competitor domain in the search bar
  2. Review Authority Score, traffic, and keywords
  3. Analyze organic vs paid traffic split
  4. Check top organic and paid keywords
  5. Review backlink profile summary

Traffic Analytics 2026

Estimate competitor website traffic and engagement:

  • Traffic Overview: Monthly visits, unique visitors, pages per visit
  • Traffic Journey: Where visitors come from and go after
  • Geographic Distribution: Traffic by country
  • Top Pages: Most visited pages on the site
  • Subdomains: Traffic to specific subdomains

Organic Research 2026

Deep dive into competitor organic performance:

Report What It Shows Use Case
Positions All keywords and rankings Find their top keywords
Position Changes Recent ranking gains/losses Spot trends and opportunities
Competitors Sites competing for same keywords Identify new competitors
Pages Top organic pages by traffic Find content to replicate

Backlink Analytics 2026

Analyze competitor link building strategies:

  • View referring domains and backlinks
  • Check Authority Score of linking sites
  • Identify anchor text patterns
  • Find new and lost backlinks
  • Use Backlink Gap to find link opportunities

Site Audit 2026

Semrush Site Audit identifies over 140 technical and on-page SEO issues. Regular audits maintain site health in 2026.

Understanding Site Health Score 2026

The health score is calculated based on errors, warnings, and notices:

  • Errors: Critical issues affecting crawling or indexing
  • Warnings: Issues that may impact rankings
  • Notices: Minor improvements and best practices

Priority Issues to Fix 2026

Issue Category Examples Priority
Crawlability Blocked pages, broken links, redirect chains Critical
HTTPS Mixed content, certificate issues High
Core Web Vitals Slow pages, layout shifts High
Indexability Duplicate content, missing canonicals Medium
Internal Linking Orphan pages, poor link structure Medium
Markup Missing or invalid schema Low

Thematic Reports 2026

Site Audit includes specialized reports:

  • Core Web Vitals: Performance metrics for each page
  • Internal Linking: Link distribution and page depth
  • Markup: Schema and structured data validation
  • Crawled Pages: Status codes, canonical tags, indexability

Position Tracking 2026

Track your keyword rankings and compare against competitors in 2026.

Setting Up Position Tracking 2026

  1. Create a new tracking campaign
  2. Add your target keywords
  3. Select target location (country, city, or ZIP code)
  4. Choose device type (desktop or mobile)
  5. Add competitors to track alongside

Position Tracking Features 2026

  • Visibility Trend: Overall search visibility over time
  • Estimated Traffic: Traffic based on rankings and CTR
  • SERP Features: Track featured snippets, PAA, local packs
  • Competitors Discovery: Automatically find new ranking competitors
  • Cannibalization Report: Identify pages competing for same keywords

Ranking Distribution 2026

Track how many keywords rank in each position range:

  • Top 3: Highest traffic potential
  • Positions 4-10: First page, good traffic
  • Positions 11-20: Striking distance opportunities
  • Positions 21-100: Long-term opportunities

FAQs: Semrush 2026

How much does Semrush cost in 2026?

Semrush pricing in 2026 starts at $129.95/month for Pro, $249.95/month for Guru, and $499.95/month for Business. Annual billing saves up to 17%. For Indian businesses, payments are in USD. The Pro plan works for freelancers and small businesses, while Guru is popular for growing marketing teams with its content marketing features.

Is Semrush worth it in 2026?

Semrush is worth it in 2026 if you need an all-in-one marketing platform. It combines SEO, PPC, content marketing, social media, and competitive research in one tool. The value depends on usage: if you use multiple features regularly, it is cost-effective. If you only need backlink analysis, specialized tools like Ahrefs may be better value.

Can I use Semrush for free in 2026?

Semrush offers a limited free plan in 2026 with 10 searches per day, access to basic features, and limited data. You can also get a 7-day free trial of Pro or Guru plans. Free users can access the basic keyword overview, domain overview, and limited site audit. For serious marketing work, a paid subscription is necessary.

What is Semrush best for in 2026?

Semrush is best for comprehensive digital marketing in 2026. Its strongest areas are: competitive analysis (seeing competitor strategies), keyword research with intent data, PPC keyword and ad research, content marketing with SEO Writing Assistant, and site audit for technical SEO. It excels as an all-in-one platform rather than specializing in one area.
