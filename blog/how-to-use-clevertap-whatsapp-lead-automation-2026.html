Setting Up CleverTap WhatsApp 2026

  1. Create a WhatsApp Business account with Meta

  3. Connect WhatsApp to CleverTap via integration settings
  4. Create and submit message templates for Meta approval

WhatsApp Business API Requirements 2026

  4. Day 3: Case study or testimonial with results
  5. Day 5: Interactive quiz to qualify (button responses)

Best Practices for CleverTap WhatsApp 2026

Message Quality 2026

  • Personalize: Use name, recent activity, and preferences in messages
  • Keep it short: WhatsApp messages should be concise and actionable
  • Use rich media: Images and videos increase engagement 2-3x
  • Include clear CTA: Every message should have a specific next step
  • Timing matters: Send during business hours in user's timezone

Compliance 2026

  • Opt-in required: Always get explicit consent before messaging
  • Easy opt-out: Provide clear unsubscribe option in every message
  • Template approval: Get all templates approved by Meta before sending
  • Frequency caps: Don't over-message—respect user attention
  • Quality rating: Monitor your WhatsApp Business quality rating

Optimization 2026

  • A/B test templates: Test different messages, images, and CTAs
  • Segment aggressively: Smaller, targeted segments outperform broad blasts
  • Monitor opt-outs: High opt-out rates signal messaging problems
  • Cross-channel coordination: Don't send WhatsApp and email about the same thing simultaneously
