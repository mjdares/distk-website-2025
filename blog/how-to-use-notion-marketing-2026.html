What Is Notion 2026?

Notion is a connected workspace that combines documents, wikis, databases, and project management in one tool. Founded in 2013, Notion has grown to serve over 30 million users in 2026. Its flexibility allows teams to build custom systems that match their exact workflows, making it popular with marketing teams of all sizes.

Notion Building Blocks 2026

Block Type Description Marketing Use
Pages Documents that can contain any content Brand guidelines, campaign briefs
Databases Structured data with properties and views Content calendar, campaign tracker
Text Blocks Paragraphs, headings, lists Documentation, SOPs
Embeds External content (videos, files, apps) Embed Figma designs, videos
Synced Blocks Content that stays synced across pages Shared headers, contact info

  • Flexibility: Build exactly what your team needs
  • All-in-One: Replace multiple tools with one workspace

  • AI Assistance: Built-in AI for writing and analysis

Getting Started with Notion 2026

Setting up Notion properly creates a solid foundation for your marketing team. Follow these steps in 2026:

Step 1: Create Your Workspace 2026

  1. Go to notion.so and sign up
  2. Create a workspace for your team
  3. Choose a workspace name and icon
  4. Invite team members
  5. Set up basic permissions

Step 2: Plan Your Structure 2026

Organize your workspace with a clear hierarchy:

  • Marketing Home: Main dashboard with links to everything
  • Wiki: Documentation, brand guidelines, processes
  • Projects: Active campaigns and initiatives
  • Content: Content calendar and assets
  • Meetings: Meeting notes and agendas
  • Resources: Templates, reference materials

Step 3: Use Teamspaces 2026

Teamspaces organize content by team or function:

  • Create a Marketing teamspace
  • Set default permissions for the teamspace
  • Add relevant pages and databases
  • Customize the sidebar for team members

Marketing Wiki Setup 2026

A marketing wiki centralizes all your team's knowledge and documentation in 2026.

Essential Wiki Pages 2026

  • Brand Guidelines: Logo usage, colors, fonts, voice
  • Messaging Framework: Value props, positioning, taglines
  • Channel Playbooks: Best practices for each channel
  • Process Documentation: How to execute common tasks
  • Tool Guides: How to use marketing tools
  • Team Directory: Who does what
  • Vendor/Partner Info: Agency contacts, contracts

Creating a Brand Guidelines Page 2026

  1. Create a new page titled "Brand Guidelines"
  2. Add a cover image and icon
  3. Use headings to organize sections (Logo, Colors, Typography)
  4. Embed or link to asset files
  5. Use callout blocks for important rules
  6. Add a table of contents for easy navigation

Wiki Best Practices 2026

  • Use consistent formatting: Create templates for common page types
  • Add owners: Assign someone responsible for each section
  • Date stamp updates: Note when pages were last reviewed
  • Use links liberally: Connect related pages
  • Enable comments: Allow questions and feedback

FAQs: Notion Marketing 2026

Is Notion free for marketing teams in 2026?

Notion offers a free plan in 2026 for individuals and small teams with unlimited pages and blocks. The free plan includes basic collaboration features. For marketing teams needing advanced permissions, admin controls, and more collaboration features, paid plans start at $10/user/month (Plus). Most marketing teams benefit from the Plus or Business plan.

How is Notion different from Airtable in 2026?

Notion and Airtable serve different purposes in 2026. Notion excels at documents, wikis, and flexible workspaces with rich text editing. Airtable is better for structured data and database-like functionality. Use Notion for documentation, meeting notes, and SOPs. Use Airtable for tracking data with multiple views. Many teams use both together.

Can Notion replace multiple marketing tools in 2026?

Notion can consolidate several marketing tools in 2026: project management (replacing Asana/Trello), documentation (replacing Google Docs), wikis (replacing Confluence), and simple databases (basic Airtable use cases). However, it cannot replace specialized tools like email marketing platforms, analytics tools, or design software. Use Notion as your central hub.

What are the best Notion templates for marketing in 2026?

Best Notion marketing templates in 2026 include: Marketing Wiki (brand guidelines, processes), Content Calendar (planning and tracking content), Campaign Tracker (managing marketing campaigns), Meeting Notes (standardized meeting documentation), and Marketing Dashboard (overview of all marketing activities). Notion's template gallery has many free options to start with.
