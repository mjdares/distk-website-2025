What Is Brand Strategy in 2026?

Brand strategy in 2026 is the deliberate plan for how your brand will be perceived, positioned, and differentiated in the market. It encompasses your brand purpose, positioning statement, messaging architecture, visual identity system, and the strategic narrative that connects with your target audience.
