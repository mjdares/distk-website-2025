Gen-3 Alpha Video Generation 2026

Text-to-Video Generation 2026

Create videos from detailed text descriptions:

"A close-up of a luxury perfume bottle on a velvet surface. Soft golden light creates reflections on the glass. Camera slowly dollies in. Shallow depth of field with bokeh in background. Elegant, premium aesthetic."

Prompt Structure for Gen-3 Alpha 2026

[Subject] + [Setting] + [Action/Movement] +
[Camera Movement] + [Lighting] + [Style/Mood]

Camera Control Keywords 2026

  • Static: Fixed camera, no movement
  • Pan: Horizontal camera rotation
  • Tilt: Vertical camera rotation
  • Dolly: Camera moves toward/away from subject
  • Tracking: Camera follows subject movement
  • Crane: Vertical camera movement
  • Orbit: Camera circles around subject

Style Keywords 2026

  • Cinematic: Film-like quality, shallow DOF
  • Commercial: Polished, professional
  • Documentary: Realistic, authentic
  • Artistic: Stylized, creative
  • Social media: Casual, relatable

Image to Video 2026

Animating Product Images 2026

Turn static product photos into video:

  1. Upload high-quality product image
  2. Select Gen-3 Alpha Image-to-Video
  3. Describe the desired motion
  4. Generate and iterate

Image-to-Video Prompt Examples 2026

Product rotation:

"The product slowly rotates 45 degrees, revealing different angles. Smooth, continuous motion. Studio lighting remains consistent."

Lifestyle animation:

"Subtle movement in the scene - steam rising from the coffee cup, gentle breeze moving the curtains, soft light flickering. Calm, cozy atmosphere."

Dynamic reveal:

"Camera pushes in dramatically toward the product. Background slightly blurs. Lighting intensifies on the subject. Cinematic reveal."

Best Practices for Image-to-Video 2026

  • High resolution: Start with quality images
  • Clear subject: Well-defined main element
  • Good lighting: Consistent, professional lighting in source
  • Simple motion: Don't over-complicate movement requests
  • Multiple attempts: Generate several versions

AI Video Editing 2026

Motion Brush 2026

Add specific movement to areas of your video:

  1. Import video or generated clip
  2. Select Motion Brush tool
  3. Paint areas you want to animate
  4. Set motion direction and intensity
  5. Generate animation

Marketing uses:

  • Animate hair, fabric, water in static shots
  • Add subtle movement to product images
  • Create cinemagraph-style content
  • Bring illustrations to life

Inpainting (Object Removal/Replacement) 2026

Remove or replace objects in video:

  1. Import video
  2. Mask the object to remove/replace
  3. For replacement: describe new object
  4. Process and review

Marketing uses:

  • Remove unwanted logos or text
  • Replace background elements
  • Clean up footage imperfections
  • Swap product variations

Background Removal 2026

  • Perfect for product videos

FAQs: Runway ML Marketing 2026

What is Runway ML used for in marketing 2026?

Runway ML is an AI-powered creative suite used for video generation and editing in marketing in 2026. Marketers use Runway for Gen-3 Alpha text-to-video generation, image-to-video animation, video editing with AI tools like motion brush and inpainting, background removal, color grading, and creating professional video content without traditional production equipment or extensive editing skills.

How much does Runway ML cost for marketing teams in 2026?

Runway ML offers tiered pricing in 2026. The free tier includes limited credits for testing. Standard plan costs approximately $15/month with 625 credits. Pro plan costs around $35/month with more credits and features. Unlimited plan at $95/month offers maximum credits for teams. Enterprise plans provide custom pricing for high-volume production needs with additional support.

What is Gen-3 Alpha and how do marketers use it in 2026?

Gen-3 Alpha is Runway's advanced text-to-video and image-to-video AI model in 2026. Marketers use Gen-3 Alpha to generate video clips from text descriptions, animate product images, create b-roll footage, produce social media content, and develop creative concepts. It offers improved temporal consistency, better human motion, and higher quality output compared to previous generations.

How does Runway ML compare to Sora for marketing video production 2026?

Runway ML and Sora serve different marketing video needs in 2026. Runway offers a complete creative suite with video editing tools, motion brush, inpainting, and multiple AI features beyond generation. Sora focuses primarily on text-to-video generation with longer duration output. Runway is better for marketers who need editing capabilities alongside generation, while Sora excels at longer, more complex scene generation.
