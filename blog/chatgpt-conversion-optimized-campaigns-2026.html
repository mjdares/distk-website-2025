Conversion-Optimised ChatGPT Campaigns in 2026: Optimising to an Event While Paying Per Click

What Is a Conversion-Optimised Campaign in ChatGPT Ads in 2026?

A conversion-optimised cost-per-click campaign, which OpenAI abbreviates as oCPC, optimises delivery toward one tracked conversion event while you continue to pay for valid clicks. In the Ads API it is set with bidding_type: "conversions". OpenAI states it is in open beta for both standard and product-feed campaigns, and it uses the same campaign and ad-group endpoints as any other campaign.

The billing shape is the reason it deserves its own guide. On most platforms, optimising to a conversion and paying per conversion travel together. Here they are deliberately separated: the platform steers toward the outcome you care about, and the invoice still counts clicks. Understanding that split is the difference between reading your results correctly and wondering why your cost per conversion does not match your bid.

Why Does Paying Per Click While Optimising to Conversions Matter?

Because the risk sits in a different place. On a pay-per-conversion model the platform carries some of the risk that clicks do not convert. On oCPC you carry it: if conversion rates fall, you still pay for every valid click. OpenAI is explicit that billing does not change to pay per conversion, that you are charged only when a valid click occurs, and that the auction determines the actual CPC.

The upside for an advertiser is transparency. You are paying for something you can see and audit in your own analytics, clicks, rather than for a conversion count that depends on the platform's attribution. The trade-off is that a broken tracking setup does not stop spend. It just makes optimisation worse while the clicks keep billing, which is why measurement has to be right before you launch.

The sentence to repeat to every client in 2026

OpenAI's guidance is to treat the CPA bid as an optimisation input, not as a conversion charge. Your cost per conversion is spend divided by conversions, and it can sit above or below the CPA bid you set, because the bid steers the auction rather than pricing the outcome.

What Does the CPA Bid Actually Control?

For an oCPC campaign, max_bid_micros on the ad group is the CPA bid, even though billing uses valid clicks. OpenAI's example: 100000000 is a 100.00 USD CPA bid in a USD account, using the standard micros scaling of 1,000,000 per major currency unit. The bid controls how the campaign competes for conversion-likely clicks.

OpenAI describes the mechanism in one sentence: oCPC uses your selected conversion event together with ad quality, relevance, click likelihood and conversion likelihood to favour clicks more likely to lead to that event. That is the decision the platform takes out of your hands. You choose the event and the CPA bid; the platform decides which clicks are worth competing for.

What Must Be True Before You Create an oCPC Campaign in 2026?

Four preconditions, and the first one is not something you can configure yourself. Skipping any of them produces either an error at creation or a campaign that optimises badly.

  1. The ad account must support conversion bidding. If campaign creation returns 403 with Conversion bidding is not enabled, OpenAI's instruction is to contact your OpenAI partner representative. This is an account-level gate.
  2. Conversion tracking must be set up with the JavaScript pixel, the Conversions API, or both. OpenAI states the Conversions API is a more reliable tracking source than the pixel alone.
  3. You need exactly one active standard conversion event to use as the optimisation goal. Custom events cannot be oCPC optimisation goals.
  4. The conversion event setting must belong to the current ad account and connect to one active conversion source.

The measurement layer behind points two to four is covered in the Measurement Pixel and Conversions API guide. If that layer is not verified, do not launch oCPC, because a campaign optimising toward a misfiring event will keep paying for clicks while learning the wrong thing.

Which Conversion Events Can You Optimise Toward?

Standard events only, and exactly one per campaign. OpenAI's examples of an optimisation goal include order_created, lead_created and registration_completed. A custom event such as a quote request can be tracked and reported on, but cannot be the thing an oCPC campaign optimises toward.

How Do You Create a Conversion-Optimised Campaign?

Create the campaign paused with bidding_type: "conversions" and the event setting attached, then create ad groups with click billing and a CPA bid, then create ads, then activate once everything is ready. OpenAI's own example uses a lifetime budget:

curl -X POST "https://api.ads.openai.com/v1/campaigns"   -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENAI_ADS_API_KEY"   -H "Content-Type: application/json"   -d '{
    "name": "Acme purchases",
    "status": "paused",
    "budget": { "lifetime_spend_limit_micros": 250000000 },
    "bidding_type": "conversions",
    "conversion_event_setting_ids": ["ces_123"]
  }'

Then the ad group, with billing_event_type set to click and the CPA bid in max_bid_micros:

curl -X POST "https://api.ads.openai.com/v1/ad_groups"   -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENAI_ADS_API_KEY"   -H "Content-Type: application/json"   -d '{
    "campaign_id": "cmpn_101",
    "name": "US English",
    "status": "active",
    "bidding_config": {
      "billing_event_type": "click",
      "max_bid_micros": 100000000
    }
  }'

Note one compatibility point from the Ads API guide. A fixed CPA bid works with either a daily or lifetime budget. If you want OpenAI to manage bids instead, using maximize_conversions, the campaign needs a daily budget, the ad group must omit max_bid_micros, and an Idempotency-Key is required when creating that ad group.

How Does oCPC Work for Product-Feed Campaigns?

The same way, using the same endpoint, and it is also in open beta. You add mode: "product_feed" and the ID of a product feed linked to the ad account alongside the conversion bidding fields. The child ad group automatically inherits the campaign's feed; you include product_set only to specify product filters, and its product_feed_id must match the campaign's feed.

curl -X POST "https://api.ads.openai.com/v1/campaigns"   -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENAI_ADS_API_KEY"   -H "Content-Type: application/json"   -d '{
    "name": "Running shoes catalog purchases",
    "status": "paused",
    "mode": "product_feed",
    "product_feed_id": "product_feed_123",
    "budget": { "lifetime_spend_limit_micros": 250000000 },
    "bidding_type": "conversions",
    "conversion_event_setting_ids": ["ces_123"]
  }'

Remember the targeting constraint from the targeting guide: new product-feed campaigns support geographic targeting and exclusions only at country level.

What Cannot Be Changed Once an oCPC Campaign Exists?

Two things, and both force a new campaign. OpenAI answers these directly in its common questions, and they shape how you name and plan campaigns from the start.

Change you might wantAllowed?What to do

Change the selected conversion eventNoCreate a new campaign optimising toward the new event
Change the campaign goalNoCreate a new campaign
Switch between a fixed CPA bid and Maximize conversionsYes, within the same objectiveUpdate the ad group's bidding_config, respecting budget rules
Adjust the CPA bid amountYesUpdate max_bid_micros on the ad group

The planning lesson: a traffic campaign that starts converting well cannot be promoted to oCPC in place. You rebuild it. Teams that expect to graduate from CPC testing to conversion bidding should name and structure the CPC campaign so its learnings transfer cleanly to the oCPC campaign that replaces it.

How Should You Judge oCPC Performance in 2026?

On conversions first, and on enough volume to mean something. OpenAI's guidance is to review impressions, clicks, conversions, spend, click-through rate and average CPC together in Ads Manager, and notes that because oCPC optimises toward the selected event, conversions are the primary outcome to review. Calculate cost per conversion yourself as spend divided by conversions.

OpenAI's own improvement list is short and worth following literally:

  • Choose the standard conversion event that best represents your campaign goal.
  • Keep conversion tracking healthy. Incomplete or incorrectly configured tracking makes reporting and optimisation less effective.
  • Use a conversion event with enough volume to evaluate performance.
  • Align ad copy with user intent and the selected conversion goal.
  • Review enough volume before making large changes to bids, budgets or creative.

Two reporting cautions carry over from the platform's general behaviour. Conversions update through daily processing, so allow at least a day before drawing conclusions. And view-through conversions are reported separately and do not feed bidding, billing or CPA, which all remain click-through-based. Judge an oCPC campaign on click-through conversions.

The volume question nobody likes to answer

OpenAI's advice to use an event with enough volume is the most important line on the page for smaller advertisers. If your purchase volume is low, optimising toward order_created gives the system very little signal. A higher-funnel standard event such as checkout_started or items_added produces more signal at the cost of optimising toward something further from revenue. Which trade-off is right depends on your volume, and that is a decision to make deliberately rather than by defaulting to the purchase event.

What Are the Common oCPC Mistakes in 2026?

  • Reading the CPA bid as the price. It is an optimisation input. You pay per valid click, and your actual cost per conversion is spend divided by conversions.
  • Launching before tracking is verified. Billing continues on clicks while optimisation learns from a misfiring event.
  • Choosing a custom event as the goal. Custom events cannot be oCPC optimisation goals.
  • Expecting to convert a CPC campaign in place. You cannot. Build a new campaign.
  • Picking an event with too little volume. OpenAI explicitly advises enough volume to evaluate performance.
  • Pairing Maximize conversions with a lifetime budget. Maximize Results needs a daily budget.
  • Treating the 403 as a bug. Conversion bidding is not enabled is an account gate; contact your OpenAI partner representative.
  • Making large changes on thin data. Review enough volume before adjusting bids, budgets or creative.

Key Questions

ChatGPT oCPC Campaigns in 2026: FAQs

Do conversion-optimised ChatGPT campaigns charge per conversion?

No. OpenAI states that billing does not change to pay per conversion. Delivery optimises toward your selected conversion event, but you are charged only when a valid click occurs, and the auction determines the actual CPC.

What does the CPA bid do in a ChatGPT oCPC campaign?

For an oCPC campaign, max_bid_micros on the ad group is the CPA bid even though billing uses valid clicks. It controls how the campaign competes for conversion-likely clicks. OpenAI says to treat it as an optimisation input rather than a conversion charge.

Why does creating an oCPC campaign return a 403 error?

If the error reads Conversion bidding is not enabled, the ad account does not yet support conversion bidding. OpenAI's instruction is to contact your OpenAI partner representative. It is an account-level gate rather than a configuration mistake.

Can I optimise a ChatGPT campaign toward a custom event?

No. An oCPC campaign supports exactly one active standard conversion event setting, and custom events cannot be used as optimisation goals. You can still track and report on custom events in clicks or impressions campaigns.

Can I switch an existing CPC campaign to conversion optimisation?

No. You cannot change an existing CPM or CPC campaign to oCPC, and you cannot change an oCPC campaign's selected conversion event after creation. Both require creating a new campaign with bidding_type set to conversions.

Is oCPC available for product-feed campaigns?

Yes, in open beta. Create the campaign with mode set to product_feed, the linked product_feed_id and bidding_type set to conversions. The ad group inherits the campaign's feed and uses billing_event_type click, and you add product_set only to filter products.
