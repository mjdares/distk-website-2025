What Is Odoo Marketing 2026?

Odoo is an open-source ERP platform with 80+ integrated business applications, including a comprehensive marketing suite. Unlike standalone marketing tools, Odoo marketing apps share data with CRM, sales, ecommerce, inventory, and accounting—providing complete visibility into customer journeys and business impact. In 2026, Odoo serves 12+ million users with both cloud and self-hosted options.

Automation Examples 2026

Welcome Series:
Trigger: New contact created
→ Wait 1 day
→ Send welcome email
→ Wait 3 days
→ Send product introduction
→ Wait 7 days
→ Send case study

Lead Nurture:
Trigger: Lead created, stage = "New"
→ Send intro email
→ Wait 5 days
→ Check: Email opened?
  → Yes: Send demo request
  → No: Send follow-up
→ Wait 7 days
→ Assign to sales

Re-engagement:
Trigger: No purchase in 90 days
→ Send "We miss you" email
→ Wait 7 days
→ Check: Opened?
  → Yes: Send special offer
  → No: Remove from active list

FAQs: Odoo Marketing 2026

What is Odoo used for in marketing 2026?

Odoo is used by businesses in 2026 for integrated marketing within a complete ERP system. Marketing apps include Email Marketing, Marketing Automation, Social Marketing, SMS Marketing, and Events. The key advantage is unified data—marketing connects directly to sales, inventory, accounting, and customer data for complete business visibility.

How much does Odoo marketing cost in 2026?

Odoo offers unique pricing in 2026. One app is free forever. Standard plan at $24.90/user/month includes all apps. Custom plan at $37.40/user/month adds Odoo Studio and multi-company. Individual marketing apps can be added incrementally. Self-hosted Community edition is free but requires technical expertise.

How does Odoo marketing differ from standalone marketing tools in 2026?

Odoo marketing differs by being part of a complete ERP system in 2026. Unlike standalone tools, Odoo marketing data flows directly to sales, inventory, and accounting. Customer purchases, support tickets, and interactions inform marketing automatically. This eliminates data silos but requires commitment to the Odoo ecosystem for full benefits.

What marketing automation can Odoo handle in 2026?

Odoo Marketing Automation in 2026 supports multi-channel automated campaigns. Create workflows triggered by customer actions, time delays, or conditions. Channels include email, SMS, and server actions. Features include A/B testing, dynamic content, and detailed analytics. While not as advanced as dedicated tools like HubSpot, it handles most SMB automation needs.
