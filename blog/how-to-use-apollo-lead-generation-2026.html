Email Template Best Practices 2026

  • Short subject lines: 4-7 words, no spam triggers
  • Personalization: Use {{first_name}}, {{company}}
  • Brief body: Under 100 words
  • Clear CTA: One specific ask
  • Mobile-friendly: Most read on mobile
  • Plain text: Better deliverability than HTML

Sample Cold Email Template 2026

Subject: Quick question about {{company}}'s [goal]

Hi {{first_name}},

Noticed {{company}} is [specific observation - hiring,
expanding, using X technology].

We help [similar companies] achieve [specific outcome]
by [brief method].

[One-sentence case study or proof point]

Worth a 15-minute call to see if there's a fit?

Best,
[Your name]

A/B Testing Sequences 2026

Test these variables:

  • Subject lines: Question vs. statement
  • Opening lines: Personalized vs. value-first
  • CTAs: Meeting vs. reply vs. resource
  • Length: Short vs. detailed
  • Timing: Morning vs. afternoon

FAQs: Apollo.io Lead Gen 2026

What is Apollo.io used for in marketing 2026?

Apollo.io is a B2B sales intelligence and engagement platform used for lead generation in 2026. Marketers and sales teams use Apollo for prospecting with its 275M+ contact database, building targeted lead lists, running email sequences, enriching CRM data, tracking buyer intent, and automating outbound campaigns. It combines data, engagement, and intelligence in one platform.

How much does Apollo.io cost for lead generation in 2026?

Apollo.io offers tiered pricing in 2026. Free plan includes 60 email credits/month and basic features. Basic plan costs $49/user/month with 900 email credits. Professional plan at $99/user/month adds unlimited emails and advanced features. Organization plan at $149/user/month includes intent data and advanced analytics. Annual billing provides discounts.

How accurate is Apollo.io contact data in 2026?

Apollo.io maintains high data accuracy in 2026 through continuous verification and AI-powered updates. Email accuracy typically ranges 85-95% depending on industry and contact type. Apollo verifies emails in real-time before sending sequences. The platform updates its database regularly and allows users to report inaccurate data. Using Apollo's verification features before campaigns improves deliverability.

How does Apollo.io compare to ZoomInfo for lead generation 2026?

Apollo.io and ZoomInfo both offer B2B databases in 2026, but differ in approach. Apollo provides an all-in-one platform with built-in email sequences at lower price points ($49-149/user). ZoomInfo offers more extensive enterprise data but at significantly higher costs ($15K+ annually). Apollo suits SMBs and mid-market teams; ZoomInfo targets enterprise with deeper firmographic data and integrations.
