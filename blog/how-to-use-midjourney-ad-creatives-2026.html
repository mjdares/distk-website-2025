Prompt Engineering for Ads 2026

Effective prompts are the key to generating high-quality ad creatives with Midjourney.

Prompt Structure for Marketing 2026

  1. Subject: What/who is in the image
  2. Setting: Environment, background, context
  3. Style: Artistic style, aesthetic, mood
  4. Lighting: Type and quality of light
  5. Camera: Angle, lens, framing
  6. Technical: Parameters for fine-tuning

Marketing Prompt Examples 2026

Product Hero Image

"Premium skincare bottle on white marble surface, soft natural window light, minimalist luxury aesthetic, product photography, shallow depth of field, 85mm lens, high-end commercial quality --ar 4:5 --style raw"

Lifestyle Ad Image

"Young Indian professional woman working on laptop in modern co-working space, natural daylight, candid authentic moment, warm color palette, editorial photography style, shot on Sony A7IV --ar 16:9"

Social Media Visual

"Abstract gradient background, soft pink to coral transition, flowing organic shapes, modern minimalist, perfect for text overlay, clean design --ar 1:1 --style raw"

E-commerce Product Shot

"Wireless earbuds floating against gradient blue background, dramatic studio lighting, tech product photography, reflections, ultra-clean, Apple-style aesthetic --ar 1:1"

Prompt Tips for Better Results 2026

  • Be specific: "young woman in her 30s" vs. "woman"
  • Describe the feeling: "warm, inviting, premium"
  • Reference photography: "editorial style", "product photography"
  • Include lighting: "soft diffused light", "dramatic rim lighting"
  • Avoid negatives: Describe what you want, not what you don't
  • Use style references: --sref parameter with reference images

Aspect Ratios for Advertising 2026

  • --ar 1:1: Instagram Feed, Facebook Feed
  • --ar 4:5: Instagram Feed (optimal), Facebook Feed
  • --ar 9:16: Stories, Reels, TikTok, Shorts
  • --ar 16:9: YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitter, Display ads
  • --ar 2:3: Pinterest
  • --ar 3:2: Website hero sections

Platform-Specific Creative 2026

Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)

  • Use 4:5 for Feed, 9:16 for Stories/Reels
  • Bright, scroll-stopping visuals
  • Leave 20% top/bottom for text safe zones
  • Human faces typically perform better

Google Display Ads

  • Multiple aspect ratios needed (1:1, 16:9, 4:1)
  • Clean backgrounds for responsive ads
  • Strong contrast for small sizes

LinkedIn Ads

  • Professional, business-appropriate imagery
  • 1:1 or 1.91:1 for Sponsored Content
  • Less lifestyle, more professional contexts

Style Control and Parameters 2026

Midjourney parameters give you precise control over output style and quality.

Essential Parameters 2026

Parameter Function Marketing Use
--ar X:Y Aspect ratio Match ad platform specs
--style raw Less Midjourney stylization More realistic, commercial look
--stylize X Artistic stylization (0-1000) Lower for products, higher for art
--sref URL Style reference image Match existing brand style
--cref URL Character reference Consistent characters across images
--no [item] Negative prompting Remove unwanted elements

Style Reference Workflow 2026

Maintain visual consistency across campaigns:

  1. Upload reference images that match your brand style
  2. Use --sref with the image URL
  3. Combine with your creative prompt
  4. Adjust --sw (style weight) from 0-1000

Quality Settings 2026

  • --q 0.25: Draft quality, fast iteration
  • --q 0.5: Lower quality, good for testing
  • --q 1: Default quality (recommended)
  • --q 2: Higher quality, more detail

Marketing Workflows 2026

Systematic workflows ensure consistent, efficient creative production.

Ad Creative Production Workflow 2026

  1. Brief: Define creative requirements, audience, message
  2. Reference: Gather style references and examples
  3. Prompt: Write initial prompts based on brief
  4. Generate: Create initial batch of images
  5. Select: Choose best candidates
  6. Vary: Generate variations of winners
  7. Upscale: Upscale final selections
  8. Post-process: Add text, logos, final touches
  9. Export: Format for each platform

Creative Testing Workflow 2026

  1. Generate 10-20 concept variations
  2. Select 5-8 strongest candidates
  3. Create variations of each (angles, lighting, subjects)
  4. Add text and branding in design tool
  5. Launch A/B tests across platforms
  6. Analyze performance data
  7. Generate more variations of winners

Batch Processing Tips 2026

  • Use consistent parameters: Save time with preset suffixes
  • Create prompt templates: [Subject] in [Setting], [Style], [Lighting] --ar [X:Y]
  • Organize by campaign: Folder structure for each project
  • Document winning prompts: Build a prompt library

Brand Consistency 2026

Maintaining brand consistency across AI-generated visuals is essential for marketing.

Brand Style Guide for Midjourney 2026

Create a Midjourney-specific style guide including:

  • Color palette: Specific colors to include/avoid
  • Photography style: Realistic, artistic, minimal, etc.
  • Lighting preferences: Natural, studio, dramatic
  • Subject guidelines: Demographics, expressions, contexts
  • Composition rules: Space for text, focal points
  • Style references: URLs to use with --sref

Consistent Character Creation 2026

For campaigns needing the same character/model:

  1. Generate initial character that matches your target
  2. Save as character reference (--cref)
  3. Use consistently across all prompts
  4. Adjust --cw (character weight) as needed

Brand Prompt Template 2026

"[Subject and action], [brand color palette], [preferred lighting], [brand photography style], [mood/feeling], [technical specs] --ar [aspect ratio] --sref [brand reference URL] --style raw"

When generating images for ads with text overlay:

FAQs: Midjourney Marketing 2026

What is Midjourney used for in marketing 2026?

Midjourney is used for AI-powered image generation in marketing in 2026. Marketers use Midjourney to create ad visuals, social media graphics, product mockups, brand imagery, hero images, email graphics, and creative concepts at scale. Midjourney generates high-quality images from text prompts, enabling rapid creative iteration and testing without traditional photoshoots or extensive design work.

How much does Midjourney cost for marketing teams in 2026?

Midjourney pricing in 2026 includes: Basic plan at $10/month with ~200 generations, Standard plan at $30/month with 15 hours of fast GPU time, Pro plan at $60/month with 30 hours fast time and stealth mode, and Mega plan at $120/month with 60 hours fast time. Most marketing teams use Standard or Pro plans. Commercial usage rights are included in all paid plans.

Can I use Midjourney images in paid ads in 2026?

Yes, Midjourney images can be used in paid ads in 2026. All paid Midjourney subscription plans include commercial usage rights, allowing you to use generated images in advertising, marketing materials, and commercial projects. However, you should disclose AI-generated content where required by advertising platform policies and avoid generating images of real public figures for advertising purposes.

How is Midjourney different from DALL-E and Stable Diffusion in 2026?

Midjourney differs from DALL-E and Stable Diffusion in 2026 in several ways. Midjourney excels at artistic, aesthetic quality with a distinctive visual style and superior composition. DALL-E 3 offers better text rendering and ChatGPT integration. Stable Diffusion provides open-source flexibility and local running options. For marketing, Midjourney is often preferred for hero images and brand visuals, while DALL-E works better for text-heavy graphics.
