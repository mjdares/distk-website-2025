Unlimited Mailboxes 2026

Mailbox Strategy 2026

Recommended Setup:

Domain Structure:
- Use secondary domains (not primary brand)
- 2-3 mailboxes per domain
- Mix of Google Workspace and Microsoft 365

Scaling Formula:
- 30-50 emails/day/account safely
- 10 accounts = 300-500 emails/day
- 50 accounts = 1,500-2,500 emails/day
- 100 accounts = 3,000-5,000 emails/day

Account Mix:
- 60% Google Workspace
- 40% Microsoft 365
- Spread across domains

Auto-Rotation 2026

  • Automatic switching: Smartlead rotates between accounts
  • Even distribution: Spreads sending load evenly
  • Health-based: Pauses underperforming accounts
  • Reply matching: Replies go to sending account
  • Campaign assignment: Specify which accounts per campaign

AI Warmup 2026

How AI Warmup Works 2026

  • Adaptive patterns: Learns optimal send times
  • Provider-specific: Different strategies for Gmail vs Outlook
  • Engagement simulation: Opens, replies, moves from spam
  • Reputation monitoring: Adjusts based on signals
  • Continuous: Never stops, even during campaigns

Warmup Configuration 2026

AI Warmup Settings:

New Accounts:
- Initial warmup emails: 5/day
- Ramp up: 2-3 per day increase
- Target: 30-40 warmup/day
- Duration: 14-21 days minimum

Ongoing:
- Keep warmup at 50% of capacity
- Never disable during campaigns
- Monitor health scores daily

Unified Inbox 2026

Inbox Features 2026

  • All replies: Every account in one view
  • Categorization: Interested, not interested, OOO, etc.
  • Quick reply: Respond from the platform
  • Lead status: Update lead stage instantly
  • Assignment: Route to team members

Managing Replies 2026

Reply Handling Workflow:

1. Review new replies (daily)
2. Categorize each reply:
   - Interested → Move to CRM, schedule follow-up
   - Not now → Add to future nurture
   - Unsubscribe → Permanent removal
   - OOO → Pause, resume later
   - Wrong person → Request referral

3. Reply from platform (maintains thread)
4. Update lead status
5. Sync to CRM if integrated
