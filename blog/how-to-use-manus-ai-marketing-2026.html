Market Research 2026

Industry Overview Task 2026

"Research the [industry] market in [region]. Compile: market size (current and projected), growth rate, key players and their market share, major trends for 2026, challenges facing the industry, and emerging opportunities. Include sources for all statistics. Output as a structured report."

Trend Research Task 2026

"Research the top trends in [industry/topic] for 2026. Find: at least 10 emerging trends, supporting data or examples for each, expert opinions or predictions, companies leading each trend, and implications for businesses. Prioritize by potential impact."

Customer Research Task 2026

"Research the typical buyer profile for [product/service type]. Find: demographic information, common pain points, decision-making factors, preferred channels, objections during purchase, and successful marketing approaches. Compile into a buyer persona document."

Market Entry Research 2026

"Research market entry requirements for [product/service] in [country/region]. Include: regulatory requirements, existing competitors, pricing benchmarks, distribution channels, cultural considerations, and successful market entry examples. Provide a market entry assessment."

Competitive Intelligence 2026

Comprehensive Competitor Analysis 2026

"Analyze our top 5 competitors in [industry]: [competitor names]. For each competitor, research: company overview, product/service offerings, pricing structure, target audience, marketing channels used, content strategy, recent news/developments, and unique selling propositions. Create a competitive matrix comparing all."

Competitor Website Audit 2026

"Audit the websites of [competitors]. Analyze: homepage messaging and value propositions, navigation structure, key landing pages, CTAs used, content types (blog, resources, case studies), social proof elements, and conversion paths. Identify best practices we should adopt."

Competitor Pricing Research 2026

"Research pricing for [product category] across major competitors. Find: pricing tiers, features at each tier, billing options (monthly/annual), free trial offerings, enterprise pricing if available, and any promotional offers. Create a pricing comparison table."

Competitor Content Analysis 2026

"Analyze the content strategy of [competitor]. Research: blog topics and frequency, downloadable resources offered, email marketing approach (sign up for newsletters if needed), social media content themes, video content, and SEO keywords they target. Identify content gaps we can exploit."

Competitor Ad Intelligence 2026

"Research the advertising approach of [competitors]. Find: active ads on Facebook Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency Center, ad copy themes, visual styles, landing pages used, and estimated ad spend if available. Compile with examples of top-performing ads."

Event Lead Research 2026

"Research upcoming [industry] conferences and events in 2026. Find: event name, date, location, expected attendees, sponsorship opportunities, speaking opportunities, and notable companies typically attending. Identify the top 10 events for lead generation potential."

Partnership Research 2026

"Identify potential partnership opportunities for [our company/product]. Research companies that: serve similar audiences, offer complementary products, have partnership programs, and have good brand reputation. For each, provide company overview, partnership potential, and contact information."

Influencer Research 2026

"Research influencers and thought leaders in [industry/topic]. Find: at least 20 relevant influencers across LinkedIn, Twitter, YouTube, and podcasts. For each: name, platform, follower count, content focus, engagement rate if visible, and contact method. Prioritize by relevance to [our audience]."

Content Brief Generation 2026

"Create a comprehensive content brief for a blog post about [topic]. Research: target keywords with search volume, competitor content (top 5 ranking), common questions about the topic, expert opinions to reference, statistics and data points, and recommended structure. Output a detailed brief."

Case Study Research 2026

"Research case studies and success stories in [industry/use case]. Find: published case studies from competitors and industry leaders, key results and metrics highlighted, storytelling approaches used, and common proof points. Compile best examples with analysis of what makes them effective."

Best Practices 2026

Writing Effective Tasks 2026

  • Be specific: Clearly define what you want
  • Set scope: Define boundaries (number of competitors, regions, etc.)
  • Specify output: Describe exact deliverable format
  • Provide context: Explain why you need this (helps agent make decisions)
  • Include examples: Show what good output looks like if helpful

Maximizing Value 2026

  • Batch similar tasks: Run related research together
  • Build on outputs: Use Manus results as input for other AI tools
  • Create templates: Develop reusable task templates for recurring needs
  • Verify important data: Spot-check critical facts and figures
  • Iterate: Refine tasks based on output quality

Quality Control 2026

  • Review outputs: Always review before using
  • Check sources: Verify citations and data points
  • Validate contacts: Confirm lead information is current
  • Update regularly: Research becomes stale; re-run periodically

Combining with Other Tools 2026

  • Manus → ChatGPT: Use research for content creation
  • Manus → Gemini: Analyze data in Google Sheets
  • Manus → Claude Code: Build automation from lead lists
  • Manus → Presentations: Turn research into decks
