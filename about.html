About Us

We build growth systems for businesses that make things.

Distk exists because distribution is the hardest part. Great products fail without it. We are a small, operator-led team that sits inside your business, connects the dots between sales, marketing, and technology, and builds systems that compound over time.

Operators, not advisors

We don't hand you a slide deck and disappear. We get into the work: setting up campaigns, writing copy, building automations, training your team. If it touches revenue, we're in the trenches with you.

Curiosity over playbooks

Every business is different. Cookie-cutter strategies produce cookie-cutter results. We start by listening, asking uncomfortable questions, and understanding the real constraints before proposing anything.

Distribution is the key

The name says it. Building a product is the beginning, not the end. We obsess over how your product reaches the right people, through the right channels, at the right time. That's the work that moves revenue.

Systems over heroics

A good month shouldn't depend on one person pulling an all-nighter. We build repeatable processes, automations, and measurement loops so your growth doesn't break when someone takes a day off.

AI as a tool, not a gimmick

We use AI where it genuinely helps: automating repetitive work, analysing data faster, scaling content production. We don't slap "AI-powered" on things for the sake of it. If a spreadsheet solves the problem, we use a spreadsheet.
