Knowledge center

Understanding GEO, AEO & Modern Search

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing content and digital presence to appear in responses generated by Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Unlike traditional SEO that focuses on search engine rankings, GEO ensures your brand is cited, referenced, and recommended in AI-generated content when users ask conversational questions.

What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

AEO focuses on capturing featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, Google AI Overviews, and other answer-focused SERP features. It involves structuring content to directly answer user questions, optimizing for voice search, and implementing schema markup so search engines can extract and display your content as the definitive answer.

How is GEO different from traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO optimizes for search engine crawlers and ranking algorithms. GEO optimizes for AI models that generate answers by synthesizing information from multiple sources. GEO requires authority building across AI training datasets, semantic content structuring, entity optimization, and creating citation-worthy content that AI systems trust and reference.

Do I still need traditional SEO if I do GEO?

Absolutely. Traditional SEO provides the foundation—domain authority, technical excellence, and content quality—that both search engines and AI models evaluate. GEO and AEO build on top of strong SEO fundamentals. Think of SEO as the base layer, with GEO and AEO as multipliers that extend your visibility into new search interfaces.

How long does it take to see GEO results?

GEO and AEO improvements can show impact in 6-12 weeks for featured snippets and answer box optimization. Full generative engine visibility across AI platforms typically takes 3-6 months as content gains authority, citations accumulate, and AI models incorporate your brand into their response patterns.

Which platforms matter most for GEO?

Priority depends on your audience, but key platforms include ChatGPT (OpenAI), Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Claude (Anthropic), Gemini (Google), Microsoft Copilot, and emerging AI search engines. For Indian markets, we optimize across platforms popular with professionals, students, and consumers researching purchases.
