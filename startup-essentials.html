Startup Essentials

Building a company is hard.
Finding the right people to help shouldn't be.

We work with startups every day. We know that company registration, compliance, finance, legal, and hiring can feel overwhelming when you're just getting started. So we built a curated list of trusted partners who can take that weight off your shoulders.

Why We Built This
First-time founders lose weeks figuring out basics

Compliance Paralysis

GST registration, ROC filings, TDS returns, PF/ESI setup. You didn't start a company to become a compliance expert. But miss a deadline and you're paying penalties before you've made revenue.

Wrong Advice, Wrong Time

A CA who handles large enterprises won't understand a bootstrapped startup's cash flow. A generalist lawyer won't know startup-specific clauses. You need people who get the stage you're at.

Hiring Without HR

Your first five hires will shape your company. But most founders have never written an offer letter, structured a compensation plan, or set up payroll. The right HR partner changes everything.

Company Registration & Compliance
Get your foundation right from day one

Private limited, LLP, OPC, or sole proprietorship. These partners handle entity formation, GST registration, MSME certification, trademark filing, and ongoing compliance so you can focus on building.
